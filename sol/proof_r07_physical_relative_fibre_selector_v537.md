## 1. Registered physical inverse system

Let `k=F3`.  On a registered cofinal ladder, let

```text
X_(n+1) --rX_n--> X_n
L_(n+1) --rL_n--> L_n
B_n : X_n -> L_n
```

be finite `k`-spaces and linear maps.  Here `X_n` is the canonical legal
instruction owner of v504: it retains one literal word/DAG, the universal
crossed-Fox record, all eleven occurrence specializations, normalized
exponents, side, boundary and localization data before the frozen physical
evaluation.  `L_n` is the complete physical constraint owner after that
fixed evaluation; it is not only one selected B3 or B4 window.

Assume

```text
rX_n is surjective,
rL_n B_(n+1) = B_n rX_n,                              (1.1)
```

and the reductions form an inverse system.  Literal materialization and
evaluation commute structurally with reduction; equality of endpoint values
alone is not used to manufacture this square.

Fix one literal base word `w0`.  Let its full signed physical residual at
level `n`, with the correction-equation sign already included, be

```text
z_n := Phi_n(w0) in L_n.                               (1.2)
```

Because the same literal word, prefixes, eleven occurrence maps and outer
evaluation are reduced functorially,

```text
rL_n z_(n+1) = z_n.                                    (1.3)
```

Thus `(z_n)` is constructed from `w0`; it is not inferred from a coarse
member and is not an additional arbitrary target-family choice.  For the
actual R07 use, (1.3) still requires the finite structural evaluator/
reduction receipt specified in Section 5.

## 2. Exact physical one-edge criterion

For one edge write `X',X,L',L,B',B,rX,rL`.  Put

```text
KX = ker(rX),       KL = ker(rL).                       (2.1)
```

Commutativity gives `B'(KX) <= KL`.

### Theorem 2.1 (physical fibre lifting)

Assume

```text
B'(KX) = KL.                                           (2.2)
```

For every compatible pair `z' in L'`, `z=rL(z')`, reduction maps the fine
solution fibre surjectively onto the coarse one:

```text
{x' : B'x'=z'} --rX--> {x : Bx=z}.                     (2.3)
```

Conversely, surjectivity in (2.3) for every `z'` implies (2.2).

#### Proof

For a coarse solution `x`, choose a source lift `sigma(x)`.  Its defect

```text
d = z' - B'sigma(x)
```

lies in `KL` by (1.1).  Under (2.2), choose `c in KX` with `B'c=d`; then
`x'=sigma(x)+c` solves the fine equation and reduces to `x`.  Conversely,
apply (2.3) to the compatible target pair `(d,0)` for arbitrary `d in KL`
and lift the coarse zero solution. QED.

This theorem is deliberately about the complete physical target.  It does
not relabel an equality after aggregation as v526's unaggregated equality.
The two statements have different target kernels.  Equation (2.2) is a
valid, possibly stronger, direct route because it covers the entire physical
kernel with source-kernel columns rather than inferring a pre-aggregation
preimage from an aggregated equality.

For one prescribed target pair, the necessary and sufficient condition is
only `d in B'(KX)`.  The full equality (2.2) is the target-independent gate
which yields a closed selector for every compatible physical fibre.

## 3. Explicit finite selector

Choose a fixed linear section `sigma:X->X'` of `rX`.  Let
`b_1,...,b_s` be a fixed basis of `KL`, and suppose A4 supplies legal
word-bearing columns

```text
C_j in KX,       B'C_j=b_j.                            (3.1)
```

For `d=sum_j d_j b_j`, define

```text
h(d)=sum_j d_j C_j,
Lift_(z')(x)=sigma(x)+h(z'-B'sigma(x)).                 (3.2)
```

Then

```text
rX h=0,       B'h=id_KL,                               (3.3)
```

and (3.2) is an explicit fine solution over `x`.  Coefficient `2` is
materialized by literal inverse, and sums use the fixed basis order.

The linear formula lives first in `X'`.  Calling it word-bearing requires
more than a vector-space section: every selected section/column has a legal
literal DAG, ordered products are retained, and direct structural replay
shows that the fine word reduces to the prescribed coarse word.  Since
`X_n` is the common universal instruction owner, the eleven occurrence
values are evaluations of that one word; they are not eleven separately
chosen preimages.

### Corollary 3.1 (finite physical cover certificate)

A directly replayed basis of `KL` together with the columns (3.1) proves
(2.2) and makes (3.2) a closed one-edge selector.  Failure or a resource cap
