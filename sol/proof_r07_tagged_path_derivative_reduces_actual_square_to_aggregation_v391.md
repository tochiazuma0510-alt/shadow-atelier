## 3. Exact aggregation-descent criterion

Let \(G_0\) be the desired coarse actor and let

### Theorem 3.1 (FULL-TARGET DESCENT IFF KERNEL VANISHES)

There exists a unique \(k[G_0]\)-linear map

\[
 B_{\rm full}:k[G_0]^r\longrightarrow L_{\rm full}
\quad\text{with}\quad
 B_{\rm full}\pi_{\rm reg}=q_{\rm full}\widehat b
\tag{3.2}
\]

if and only if both of the following conditions hold:

\[
 \boxed{
 q_{\rm full}\widehat b(\ker\pi_{\rm reg})=0,}
\tag{3.3}
\]

and the induced map in (3.2) is \(k[G_0]\)-linear.  A sufficient finite
form of these conditions is

\[
 \boxed{

 =\pi(g)q_{\rm full}(w)
 \quad(g\in\Delta^{\rm act},\ w\in W_C),}
\tag{3.4}
\]

\[
 B_{\rm full}(\bar e_a)

\tag{3.5}

#### Proof

If (3.2) exists, it kills \(\ker\pi_{\rm reg}\), giving (3.3), and its
induced map is linear by hypothesis.  Conversely, (3.3) makes

Linearity gives (3.2), hence (3.3). \(\square\)
