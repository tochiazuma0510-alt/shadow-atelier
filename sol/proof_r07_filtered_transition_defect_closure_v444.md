# R07: filtered transition-defect closure without restarting the actor orbit (v444)

Author: Sol / 2026-09-03

Status: candidate algebraic theorem.  It gives an exact reusable actor-closure
certificate from one filtration precision to the next and preserves literal
ancestry.  Applied with v441 and the coordinate formulas of v443, it removes
the need to rebuild the whole previously known orbit at every positive grade.
It does not prove that an actual residual is in the resulting image and does
not declare A0, COMMON, a cofinal lift, fake, or Ihara. `verified=false`.

## 1. Compatible occurrence modules

Let (k=\mathbf F_3).  For (d\geq0), let

\[
 r_d:\widehat{\mathcal O}_{d+1}\twoheadrightarrow
       \widehat{\mathcal O}_d                            \tag{1.1}
\]

be a compatible filtered reduction of finite-dimensional modules.  The hat
means that every non-filtration coordinate required by the problem is already
included: in particular normalized exponent, PB3 central augmentation, and
all six occurrence tags are retained with their correct reductions.  Thus a
vector in (ker r_d) has zero lower and auxiliary coordinates and lives in
the new grade.

Let (Gamma) be generated by the four registered correlated source actors

\[
 \mathcal A=\{x,x^{-1},y,y^{-1}\}.                    \tag{1.2}
\]

Each actor acts linearly on every
(widehat{\mathcal O}_d), commutes with (1.1), and acts trivially on the
normalized-exponent coordinates.  Let
(s_1^{(d)},\ldots,s_m^{(d)}) be the compatible evaluations of the original
literal compact seeds; in R07, (m=44).  Define the complete legal
occurrence image

\[
 U_d=\operatorname{span}_k\{\gamma s_a^{(d)}:
          \gamma\in\Gamma, 1\leq a\leq m\}.          \tag{1.3}
\]

This construction occurs before physical aggregation.  No action on an
aggregated physical quotient is used.

## 2. What one exhausted closure must retain

Suppose an exhausted deterministic closure at precision (d) has produced
an ordered basis

\[
 B_d=(b_1,\ldots,b_r)                                \tag{2.1}
\]

of (U_d).  Every (b_i) carries literal instruction-tree ancestry.  In
addition to the basis, retain the exact reduction records

\[
 s_a^{(d)}=\sum_i q_{ai}b_i                           \tag{2.2}
\]

for every original seed, and

\[
 A b_i=\sum_j q_{Aij}b_j                              \tag{2.3}
\]

for every (A\in\mathcal A) and every (i).  Sparse pivot-reduction DAGs
are enough; the coefficient vectors need not be flattened or duplicated.

Queue exhaustion plus (2.2)--(2.3) is a finite presentation of the generated
module for the next-step purpose.  Persisting only the accepted basis rows,
only their physical aggregates, or only the coefficients of one target
solution is not enough.

## 3. Lifted relation defects

Re-evaluate the same literal ancestry of (b_i) at precision (d+1), and
write the resulting canonical lift as (widetilde b_i).  Define the seed
and transition defects

\[
 \epsilon_a=s_a^{(d+1)}-\sum_iq_{ai}\widetilde b_i,   \tag{3.1}
\]

\[
 \delta_{A,i}=A\widetilde b_i-
                  \sum_jq_{Aij}\widetilde b_j.       \tag{3.2}
\]

By (1.1)--(2.3), every vector in (3.1)--(3.2) belongs to
(ker r_d).  Let

\[
 H_d=\operatorname{span}_k\{\gamma\epsilon_a,
                    \gamma\delta_{A,i}:\gamma\in\Gamma\}
       \subseteq\ker r_d.                            \tag{3.3}
\]

The closure in (3.3) takes place only in the new-grade module.  In the two
R07 (C_3^3) extensions, its actor arithmetic is the associated-grade part
of v443's exact truncated formulas.

## 4. Exact transition-defect theorem

### Theorem 4.1

With the hypotheses above,

\[
 \boxed{
 U_{d+1}=\operatorname{span}_k
          (\widetilde b_1,\ldots,\widetilde b_r)\oplus H_d.}   \tag{4.1}
\]

In particular, an exact occurrence closure at precision (d+1) is obtained
by lifting the old basis once and closing only the seed and actor-transition
defects in the new grade.  No old actor path has to be rediscovered.

#### Proof

Let (V) denote the right side of (4.1) before asserting directness.  Every
(widetilde b_i) is the evaluation of its literal ancestry, hence belongs to
(U_{d+1}).  Equations (3.1)--(3.2) show that every initial defect is a
linear combination of elements of (U_{d+1}).  Since (U_{d+1}) is
(Gamma)-stable, (H_d\subseteq U_{d+1}), so (V\subseteq U_{d+1}).

Conversely, (3.1) writes every seed (s_a^{(d+1)}) in (V).  Equation
(3.2) shows that each generator (A\in\mathcal A) sends every
(widetilde b_i) into (V), while (3.3) makes (H_d) generator-stable.
Thus (V) is a (Gamma)-stable subspace containing all seeds.  The
minimality in (1.3) gives (U_{d+1}\subseteq V).

Finally, a combination of the (widetilde b_i) lying in (H_d) reduces to
a combination of the basis (b_i) equal to zero.  All its coefficients are
zero.  Hence the sum is direct. (square)

### Corollary 4.2

The rank increment is exactly

\[
 \dim U_{d+1}-\dim U_d=\dim H_d.                     \tag{4.2}
\]

A transition-defect producer may therefore report progress as
`old rank + new-grade rank`; it must not call a partially closed (H_d)
exhausted.

## 5. Word-bearing induction through all six grades

Every defect (3.1)--(3.2) has literal ancestry: substitute the stored
instruction tree of each (b_i), prepend the one actor in (3.2), and retain
the signed lower-reduction DAG.  Actor closure of these defects preserves
that ancestry.  After echelonizing (H_d), concatenate the lifted old basis
and the new-grade basis to form (B_{d+1}), and record new versions of
(2.2)--(2.3).  Theorem 4.1 then applies again.

Induction gives one compatible finite instruction database through degrees
(0,1,\ldots,6).  The database is versioned by precision; an entry is never
promoted merely because its lower reduction vanished.  Literal evaluation,
not a copied coefficient row, supplies every higher lift.

This is a finite filtered coherence statement.  It is not the stronger
inverse-limit assertion that the same finite roster is strictly onto on every
future cofinal refinement.  That later statement remains the all-depth gate
of v397/v400.

## 6. Interface with the physical fibre test

Apply physical aggregation only after (4.1) has produced the complete
occurrence image.  The lifted old basis can have new-grade components even
when its lower physical images are dependent, while (H_d) is already zero
in all lower occurrence coordinates.  Therefore process

\[
 \{\widetilde b_i\}_i\cup\{h_j\}_j                  \tag{6.1}
\]

through v441's lower-first/fibre echelon, where ({h_j}) is an exhausted
basis of (H_d).  The resulting zero-lower grade rows span exactly v441's
canonical image fibre (K_d).

One must not replace (6.1) by only the new-grade rows (h_j): physical
dependencies among the lifted old basis can expose additional grade
directions.  Conversely, one need not regenerate every historical actor word:
Theorem 4.1 proves that the finite transition defects already contain all
ways in which a formerly dependent source row can become new at the higher
precision.

For a MEMBER residual, the fibre solve returns a literal update and its
next residual.  For NONMEMBER, a dual must annihilate the entire fibre formed
from (6.1), not just (H_d).

## 8. Certificate gates

At each precision a transition-defect certificate must bind:

1. the exact filtered module, reduction, seed roster, four actor maps and
   literal evaluator;
2. the complete old basis with literal ancestry;
3. all (m) seed reductions (2.2) and all (4r) actor transitions (2.3);
4. direct lower-level replay of every recorded equality;
5. direct proof that every lifted defect reduces to zero;
6. an exhausted new-grade closure of all defects and its rank;
7. direct equality (4.1), checked by seed containment, generator stability,
   reduction to the old basis, and the direct-sum rank identity;
8. the complete lower-first physical fibre, actual residual, and either its
   literal preimage or separating dual.

A positive direct literal target replay may certify the resulting MEMBER
without a second implementation reproducing every discovery pivot.  In that
case pivot order and intermediate ranks remain telemetry; the transition
equalities, literal output, target equality, and next residual remain
load-bearing and must be independently replayed.
