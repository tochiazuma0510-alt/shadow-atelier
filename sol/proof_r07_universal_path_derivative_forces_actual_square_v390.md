## 2. Factorization through the one-common-word actual image

\[

#### Proof

coordinate, and separation of the inverse limit gives
\(\mathfrak B(v)=0\).  Hence \(\mathfrak B\) factors uniquely through its
image \(A_C^{\rm enr}=\operatorname{im}\tau\), proving (2.2)--(2.3).
