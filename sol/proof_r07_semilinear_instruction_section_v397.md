## 1. Free instructions before coefficients are identified

Put \(k=\mathbf F_3\).  At one adjacent edge of the registered tower, let

\[
 r_D:D_1\longrightarrow D_0,
 \qquad r_L:L_1\longrightarrow L_0,
 \qquad B_i:D_i\longrightarrow L_i                         \tag{1.1}
\]

be the legal correction spaces, actual residual spaces, and literal
eleven-occurrence first-difference maps of v395.  We use only the
occurrencewise naturality square

\[
 r_LB_1=B_0r_D.                                             \tag{1.2}
\]

No common action of a group ring on the eleven occurrences is assumed.

Let \(\mathcal T\) be a finite registered roster of typed instruction
trees.  A tree records its correction leaf, every actor path, conjugation,
commutator, inverse, occurrence tag, and physical aggregation order.  Let

\[
 U_1=k^{(\mathcal T)}                                      \tag{1.3}
\]

be the free vector space on those literal trees.  Evaluation and residual
evaluation are the linear maps

\[
 e_1:U_1\longrightarrow D_1,
 \qquad q_1:=B_1e_1:U_1\longrightarrow L_1.                \tag{1.4}
\]

Thus two trees are not identified merely because a prematurely collapsed
coefficient formula makes them look alike.  In particular
\((p-1)\delta(a)\) and \((a-1)\delta(p)\) remain different source
instructions unless literal evaluation proves an equality.

The correct relative instruction space is the pulled-back kernel

\[
 U_1^{\mathrm{rel}}
 :=\{u\in U_1:r_De_1(u)=0\}.                               \tag{1.5}
\]

It is computed by ordinary linear equations on evaluated word-bearing
columns.  From (1.2),

\[
 q_1(U_1^{\mathrm{rel}})\subseteq\ker r_L.                 \tag{1.6}
\]

Definition (1.5) is important: it requires neither a formal group-ring
augmentation ideal nor an equality between formal and physical
coinvariants.

## 2. Exact class-specific section

Let \(C_1\subseteq\ker r_L\) be the finite-dimensional actual defect space
which is to be corrected.  It may be the span of the defects generated by
the fixed class

\[
 \chi_{07}=[x,y][y,z]^{-1},                                \tag{2.1}
\]

rather than the whole of \(\ker r_L\).  Put

\[
 V_1:=\{u\in U_1^{\mathrm{rel}}:q_1(u)\in C_1\},
 \qquad q_{1,C}:=q_1|_{V_1}:V_1\longrightarrow C_1.        \tag{2.2}
\]

This restriction loses no correction of a vector in \(C_1\): every
preimage of such a vector already belongs to \(V_1\).

### Theorem 2.1 (PIVOTED INSTRUCTION SECTION)

The following are equivalent.

1. Every defect in \(C_1\) has a legal word-bearing relative correction.
2. \(C_1\subseteq q_1(U_1^{\mathrm{rel}})\).
3. For one, hence every, basis \(b_1,\ldots,b_s\) of \(C_1\), each
   augmented system

   \[
      q_1(u)=b_j,\qquad u\in U_1^{\mathrm{rel}}             \tag{2.3}
   \]

   is consistent.

When these conditions hold, fix an ordering of \(\mathcal T\), row-reduce
once, and choose the lexicographic pivot solution \(u_j\) of (2.3).  The
map

\[
 \sigma_1:C_1\longrightarrow V_1,
 \qquad \sigma_1\!\left(\sum_j a_jb_j\right)=\sum_j a_ju_j \tag{2.4}
\]

is a linear section, and

\[
 \boxed{h_1=e_1\sigma_1:C_1\longrightarrow\ker r_D,
 \qquad B_1h_1=1_{C_1}.}                                  \tag{2.5}
\]

#### Proof

The equivalence is the definition of containment in the image of a linear
map.  Under (2), choose the displayed solutions and extend linearly.  Then
\(q_1\sigma_1=1_{C_1}\).  Definition (1.5) gives
\(e_1\sigma_1(C_1)\subseteq\ker r_D\), while (1.4) gives

\[
 B_1e_1\sigma_1=q_1\sigma_1=1_{C_1}.
\]

This is (2.5). \(\square\)

For one recursively produced defect \(\beta_1\), the exact gate is only

\[
 \boxed{\beta_1\in q_1(U_1^{\mathrm{rel}}).}               \tag{2.6}
\]

A solved coordinate vector \(u\) is already its literal instruction
certificate, and v395 gives

\[
 c_1=\widetilde c_1+e_1(u).                                \tag{2.7}
\]

### Remark 2.2 (why v396 asked for too much)

The formula \(\phi_D\phi_L^{-1}\) in v396 tried to use *every*
coefficient representative of a target orbit interchangeably.  That
formula needs equality of source and target relation modules.  The pivoted
section (2.4) instead chooses one representative for each target basis
vector.  A linear surjection of vector spaces always admits such a section,
even when

\[
 \ker q_1\not\subseteq\ker e_1.                            \tag{2.8}
\]

Consequently orbit freeness and relation equality are sufficient shortcuts,
not necessary gates.  Likewise (1.5) makes the physical relative condition
part of the solve, so the separate orbit-saturation equality of v396 is not
needed.  What remains load-bearing is the actual image containment (2.6)
and the occurrencewise square (1.2).

## 3. The dual alternative and translated-column generation

Put \(W_1=q_1(U_1^{\mathrm{rel}})\subseteq\ker r_L\).  In finite
dimension, exactly one of the following holds:

\[
 \begin{array}{ll}
 \text{(lift)}& C_1\subseteq q_1(U_1^{\mathrm{rel}}),\\[2mm]
 \text{(separator)}&
 \exists\,\lambda\in(\ker r_L)^*:\
 \lambda(W_1)=0\ \hbox{ and }\ \lambda(C_1)\ne0.
 \end{array}                                               \tag{3.1}
\]

For a pointed defect \(\beta_1\), failure is witnessed more precisely by

\[
 \lambda q_1(U_1^{\mathrm{rel}})=0,
 \qquad \lambda(\beta_1)\ne0.                             \tag{3.2}
\]

This is ordinary finite-dimensional duality.  It also explains the current
full-boundary column generation.  Suppose a base boundary term has left and
right labels \(g,h\).  A translated column is determined by the relative
translation

\[
 t=gh^{-1}.                                                \tag{3.3}
\]

Pairing the current \(\lambda\) with the finitely many terms and collecting
equal \(t\)'s computes all scalars

\[
 a_t=\lambda\bigl(q_1(T_t)\bigr)                           \tag{3.4}
\]

without materializing every dense column.  If some \(a_t\ne0\), then
\(T_t\) is an explicit missing instruction column which kills the current
separator or raises the image rank.  Only those nonzero columns need full
replay.  If every registered \(t\) has \(a_t=0\), this is a separator only
after the roster of translations and instruction types has been proved
exhaustive.  Thus an empty heuristic candidate list is not a negative
certificate, whereas an exhaustive finite correlation table is.

## 4. One continuous section on all refinements

Now let \(U^{\mathrm{rel}},D_{\mathrm{rel}},L,C\) be the completed
inverse-limit instruction source, legal relative correction source, actual
residual target, and class-defect target.  Equip them with their
inverse-limit filtrations and assume
that they are complete, separated, linearly compact \(k\)-spaces with
finite-dimensional graded pieces.  Literal evaluation and residual
evaluation give continuous filtered maps.  Restrict to the closed
class-bearing source

\[
 U_C^{\mathrm{rel}}:=\{u\in U^{\mathrm{rel}}:Be(u)\in C\},
 \qquad e:U_C^{\mathrm{rel}}\longrightarrow D_{\mathrm{rel}},
 \qquad q:=Be:U_C^{\mathrm{rel}}\longrightarrow C.        \tag{4.1}
\]

Assume the single strict-surjectivity condition

\[
 \boxed{q(\mathcal F^rU_C^{\mathrm{rel}})
       =\mathcal F^rC\quad\text{for every }r.}              \tag{4.2}
\]

### Theorem 4.1 (STRICT OCCURRENCEWISE INSTRUCTION LIFT)

Under (4.2), there is one continuous filtered linear section

\[
 \sigma:C\longrightarrow U_C^{\mathrm{rel}},
 \qquad q\sigma=1_C.                                      \tag{4.3}
\]

Consequently

\[
 \boxed{h=e\sigma:C\longrightarrow D_{\mathrm{rel}},
 \qquad Bh=1_C}                                           \tag{4.4}
\]

is a continuous, word-bearing relative homotopy on every refinement at
once.  Its finite reductions can be constructed by compatible pivot lifts;
no unrelated family of finite-stage choices is required.

#### Proof

Apply v357 Lemma 2.1 to the strict surjection (4.2).  Its proof chooses
sections on successive finite filtered quotients and changes each new
section by a kernel-valued map so that it reduces to the preceding one.
The inverse limit is (4.3).  Composition with literal evaluation gives
(4.4), exactly as in Theorem 2.1. \(\square\)

The hypothesis is not an assertion that one successful finite stage
automatically propagates.  It says exactly what must be proved to make that
propagation valid: the same registered instruction roster must cover every
new graded defect layer.  Equivalently, every finite quotient must pass the
image test with compatible filtration rank.

## 5. Base transport does not restore common-action linearity

Let \(B_0\) be the first-difference operator at the arithmetic base, and let
\(B_1=B_0+T\) be the operator after applying a relative correction.  By
v357 Theorem 1.1, \(T\) is the literal sum of eleven occurrence-specific
prefix-ratio terms and raises filtration once.  Assume only

\[
 T e(U_C^{\mathrm{rel}})\subseteq C.                       \tag{5.1}
\]

For a section \(\sigma\) of \(q=B_0e\), put

\[
 K=\sigma Te:U_C^{\mathrm{rel}}\longrightarrow
       \mathcal F^1U_C^{\mathrm{rel}}.                    \tag{5.2}
\]

Then the Neumann series converges and

\[
 \boxed{e_T=e(1+K)^{-1},
 \qquad (B_0+T)e_T=q.}                                    \tag{5.3}
\]

Indeed, \(qK=Te\), so
\((B_0+T)e=q(1+K)\), and right composition with
\((1+K)^{-1}\) proves (5.3).  This uses only \(k\)-linearity and
filtration gain.  The eleven actions may remain semilinear and different.
In particular

\[
 \boxed{h_T=e_T\sigma:C\longrightarrow D_{\mathrm{rel}},
 \qquad (B_0+T)h_T=1_C}                                   \tag{5.4}
\]

is the transported word-bearing right inverse.

Thus the same strict instruction cover survives the actual moving base.
Together with the localized higher-remainder hypotheses of v319 and the
first-remainder cancellation of v356, it supplies the linear homotopy used
by the all-depth Newton recursion.  Those localization and materialization
hypotheses are not reproved here.
