## 3. Exact lazy affine quotient

For (r=3,4), put (G_r=E_{r,0}) and let

\[
 C_{1,r}=\mathbf F_3[G_r]^{d_r},\qquad
 D_{0,r}=\mathbf F_3[G_r]
 \langle\delta_0(r_{r,j})\rangle.
 \tag{3.1}
\]

An encountered value of (E_{r,1}) is represented by a raw pair

\[
 (c,g)\in C_{1,r}\rtimes G_r,
 \tag{3.2}
\]

with

\[
 (c,g)(d,h)=(c+g d,gh),\qquad
 (c,g)^{-1}=(-g^{-1}c,g^{-1}).
 \tag{3.3}
\]

The marked generator (a_i) is the pair

\[
 \widetilde a_i=(e_i,q(a_i)).
 \tag{3.4}
\]

Only the subgroup generated by (3.4) is used.  The ambient semidirect product
is never declared to be the successor.

Two pairs are equal exactly when their base blobs agree and their chain
difference belongs to (D_{0,r}).  A persistent rank-zero-replayable echelon
may accumulate translated boundary rows across equality queries.  For a
nonzero remainder it forms an exact dual and applies the complete task179
support-times-occurrence correlation.  Every ACTIVE translated row may be
added in canonical batch order.  The query terminates in exactly one of:

* equality, with a literal coefficient chain reconstructing the difference;
* inequality, with a nonzero dual pairing and a complete zero correlation
  against both/all eleven translated relator families of the given type; or
* `UNKNOWN_RESOURCE`.

A sampled orbit is never inequality.  Checkpoints retain and replay every
retained/dependent row, correlation, dual, query cursor, and affine-label
decision from rank zero.

For canonical interning, compare a new raw pair against prior labels having
the same base blob in first-encounter order.  Record the positive merge chain
or every preceding negative separator.  If no earlier label is equal, create
one new label.  This constructs only the marked subgroup values actually
encountered in the words below.
