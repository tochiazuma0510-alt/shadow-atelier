# R07 relative marginal Hensel selector (v314)

Author: Sol / 2026-08-29

Status: paper theorem specializing the pointed Hensel principle to v313's
common-source marginal problem.  It identifies the exact obstruction to
lifting one already chosen marginal coefficient across one refinement, gives
a finite literal column roster and a complete dual alternative, and states
the precise uniform hypothesis under which one successful level propagates
through every relative pro-3 refinement.  The actual R07 marginal targets
and obstruction classes have not been computed.  No compatible lift, fake
certificate, or Ihara witness is declared.

## 1. A matched marginal edge

Put \(k=\mathbf F_3\).  At level \(n\), retain v313's finite common-source
map, including every registered linear side coordinate,

\[
 \mathcal T_n:A_n=k[\Gamma_n]\longrightarrow W_n,
 \qquad t_n\in W_n,
 \qquad X_n=\{a\in A_n:\mathcal T_n(a)=t_n\}.
\tag{1.1}
\]

The space \(W_n\) contains the zero first-shadow marginal and all
prefix-corrected local quotient coordinates
\(Q_{b,n}=k[G_{b,n}]/H_{b,n}\).  No componentwise replacement of the common
source is allowed.

For one matched refinement assume surjective linear reductions

\[
 u_n:A_{n+1}\twoheadrightarrow A_n,
 \qquad v_n:W_{n+1}\twoheadrightarrow W_n
\tag{1.2}
\]

such that

\[
 v_n\mathcal T_{n+1}=\mathcal T_nu_n,
 \qquad v_n(t_{n+1})=t_n.
\tag{1.3}
\]

The second surjectivity is convenient for notation but is not used outside
the kernel containing the actual defect.  The load-bearing assumptions are
the commuting square and target compatibility.  They require the literal
prefix triples \((\epsilon_b,p_b,r_b)\), the cyclic ambiguity spaces
\(H_{b,n}\), and all side rows to reduce naturally.

Define the vertical source and target spaces

\[
 K_n=\ker u_n,
 \qquad Z_n=\ker v_n,
 \qquad R_n=\mathcal T_{n+1}(K_n)\leq Z_n.
\tag{1.4}
\]

The inclusion follows from (1.3).

## 2. The pointed marginal obstruction

Fix \(a_n\in X_n\), and choose any linear lift
\(\widetilde a_n\in A_{n+1}\) with
\(u_n(\widetilde a_n)=a_n\).  Its upper residual is

\[
 \delta_n(\widetilde a_n)
 =t_{n+1}-\mathcal T_{n+1}(\widetilde a_n).
\tag{2.1}
\]

Equation (1.3) and \(a_n\in X_n\) show that
\(\delta_n(\widetilde a_n)\in Z_n\).  Put

\[
 \boxed{
 \omega_n(a_n)=
 [\delta_n(\widetilde a_n)]\in Z_n/R_n.}
\tag{2.2}
\]

### Lemma 2.1 (LIFT-INDEPENDENCE)

The class \(\omega_n(a_n)\) is independent of the chosen lift
\(\widetilde a_n\).

#### Proof

Two lifts differ by \(k\in K_n\).  Replacing \(\widetilde a_n\) by
\(\widetilde a_n+k\) changes (2.1) by
\(-\mathcal T_{n+1}(k)\in R_n\).  \(\square\)

### Theorem 2.2 (POINTED MARGINAL HENSEL CRITERION)

For the fixed downstairs solution \(a_n\), the following are equivalent.

1. There is \(a_{n+1}\in X_{n+1}\) with
   \(u_n(a_{n+1})=a_n\).
2. \(\omega_n(a_n)=0\).
3. For one, hence every, lift \(\widetilde a_n\), there is \(k_n\in K_n\)
   such that

   \[
    \mathcal T_{n+1}(k_n)=\delta_n(\widetilde a_n).
   \tag{2.3}
   \]

When (2.3) is retained with coefficient ancestry, an explicit upper solution
is

\[
 \boxed{a_{n+1}=\widetilde a_n+k_n.}
\tag{2.4}
\]

#### Proof

The equivalence of 2 and 3 is the definition of \(R_n\).  If (2.3) holds,
then (2.1) gives

\[
 \mathcal T_{n+1}(\widetilde a_n+k_n)=t_{n+1},
\]

and \(u_n(k_n)=0\), proving 1.  Conversely, if \(a_{n+1}\) is a lift in
\(X_{n+1}\), then
\(k_n=a_{n+1}-\widetilde a_n\in K_n\) satisfies (2.3).  \(\square\)

This is a pointed criterion.  It asks whether the one residual created by
the already selected coefficient is correctable; it does not require
\(R_n=Z_n\).

## 3. Finite literal roster and complete dual alternative

Assume the map \(u_n\) in (1.2) is induced by a finite group quotient

\[
 \Gamma_{n+1}\twoheadrightarrow\Gamma_n,
 \qquad N_n=\ker(\Gamma_{n+1}\to\Gamma_n).
\tag{3.1}
\]

Choose literal words \(s_1,\ldots,s_r\) generating \(N_n\) as a group.

### Lemma 3.1 (VERTICAL GROUP-ALGEBRA KERNEL)

\[
 \boxed{
 K_n=\sum_{i=1}^r k[\Gamma_{n+1}](s_i-1),
 \qquad
 R_n=\operatorname{span}_k\{
 \mathcal T_{n+1}(g(s_i-1)):
 g\in\Gamma_{n+1},\ 1\leq i\leq r\}.}
\tag{3.2}
\]

The translations in the second equality are performed in the **source**
group algebra before applying \(\mathcal T_{n+1}\).  This distinction is
load-bearing: v313's quotients \(Q_{b,n}\) are vector-space quotients, and
their ambiguity spaces need not be stable under an ambient left action.

#### Proof

The kernel of the group-algebra quotient is generated by \(g-1\) for
\(g\in N_n\).  If \(g=t_1\cdots t_m\), with each
\(t_j\in\{s_i^{\pm1}\}\), then

\[
 g-1=\sum_{j=1}^m t_1\cdots t_{j-1}(t_j-1),
 \qquad s_i^{-1}-1=-s_i^{-1}(s_i-1).
\tag{3.3}
\]

This proves the first equality.  Apply the linear common-source map
\(\mathcal T_{n+1}\) after expanding the source coefficients in the group
basis to obtain the second.  No action on the quotients \(Q_{b,n+1}\) is
used.  \(\square\)

Since all spaces at a finite edge are finite dimensional, exact row
reduction gives a complete dichotomy:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{MEMBER:}&
 \delta_n=\sum_{g,i}c_{g,i}\,
 \mathcal T_{n+1}(g(s_i-1)),\\[2mm]
\text{NONMEMBER:}&
 \lambda_n(R_n)=0,\quad
 \lambda_n(\delta_n)\ne0
 \quad\text{for some }\lambda_n\in Z_n^*.
\end{array}
\tag{3.4}
\]

In the MEMBER case the same coefficients give the literal correction

\[
 k_n=\sum_{g,i}c_{g,i}g(s_i-1).
\tag{3.5}
\]

In the NONMEMBER case \(\lambda_n\) is a complete obstruction to every
upper solution reducing to the fixed \(a_n\), not merely to the chosen
lift.  The conclusion follows from finite-dimensional separation of the
proper subspace \(R_n\) from \(\delta_n\).  Thus an authenticated finite
edge never needs to report a bounded-search `NO`: it returns literal MEMBER,
complete-dual NONMEMBER, or a resource/input UNKNOWN before finishing the
closure.

## 4. The exact all-refinement selector

Choose a deterministic linear section
\(\sigma_n:A_n\to A_{n+1}\) of \(u_n\).  For \(a\in X_n\), put

\[
 d_n(a)=t_{n+1}-\mathcal T_{n+1}(\sigma_n(a))\in Z_n.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Let \(D_n\leq Z_n\) be a registered subspace containing all actual values
of (4.1) on which a selector is claimed.

### Theorem 4.1 (RELATIVE MARGINAL HENSEL SELECTOR)

Suppose there is a word-bearing linear map

\[
 h_n:D_n\longrightarrow K_n,
 \qquad
 \mathcal T_{n+1}h_n=1_{D_n}.
\tag{4.2}
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{
 L_n(a)=\sigma_n(a)+h_n(d_n(a))}
\tag{4.3}
\]

is an explicit lift in \(X_{n+1}\) of every
\(a\in X_n\) with \(d_n(a)\in D_n\).

If (4.2) is supplied uniformly at every matched refinement and contains the
successive actual residuals, then starting from one \(a_0\in X_0\), the
recursion

\[
 a_{n+1}=L_n(a_n)
\tag{4.4}
\]

constructs one compatible solution at every level and hence one completed
coefficient \(a_\infty\in\varprojlim A_n\).  No independent levelwise
choice and no later compactness selection is required.

#### Proof

By (4.2),

\[
 \mathcal T_{n+1}L_n(a)
 =\mathcal T_{n+1}\sigma_n(a)+d_n(a)=t_{n+1}.
\]

Because \(h_n(d_n(a))\in K_n\), reduction of (4.3) is \(a\).  Induction
gives (4.4), and its compatibility defines the inverse-limit element.
\(\square\)

There are two useful strengths of the hypothesis:

1. **full relative surjectivity:** \(R_n=Z_n\), which lifts every
   downstairs solution; and
2. **actual-class surjectivity:** only the named residual
   \(d_n(a_n)\) lies in \(R_n\), with a retained preimage.

The second is enough for one explicit R07 branch.  One successful finite
level propagates to all levels only after a uniform family (4.2), or the
weaker actual-class preimage at every edge, has been proved.  The finite
success alone still does not imply this.

## 5. Return split and the generalized relative-dihedral formula

Assume the return involution \(\theta\) commutes with the reductions and
with every \(\mathcal T_n\).  Since the characteristic is three, put

\[
 e_-=(1-\theta)/2,
 \qquad e_+=(1+\theta)/2.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Then \(K_n,Z_n,R_n\), and the obstruction (2.2) split into return-odd and
return-even parts.  Suppose the typed relative-dihedral theorem supplies

\[
 h_{-,n}:D_{-,n}\longrightarrow e_-K_n,
 \qquad \mathcal T_{n+1}h_{-,n}=1
\tag{5.2}
\]

on the required odd marginal defects.  The only remaining linear edge
condition for the actual branch is

\[
 \boxed{
 e_+d_n(a_n)\in
 \mathcal T_{n+1}(e_+K_n).}
\tag{5.3}
\]

One literal preimage
\(h_{+,n}(e_+d_n(a_n))\) is enough; a right inverse on the whole even
module is not required.  The actual correction is

\[
 \boxed{
 k_n=h_{-,n}(e_-d_n(a_n))
       +h_{+,n}(e_+d_n(a_n)).}
\tag{5.4}
\]

Consequently the sought infinite-refinement generalization of the relative
dihedral lift has the exact form

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{dihedral odd vertical section}
 \quad\oplus\quad
 \text{actual-even marginal Hensel preimage}.}
\tag{5.5}
\]

This is v310's class-specific second homotopy expressed at every refinement
edge.  It is also the missing nonemptiness mechanism in v313: (5.4) makes
the bonding maps along the selected branch explicitly nonempty instead of
assuming nonemptiness and invoking Koenig compactness afterwards.

## 6. R07 certificate boundary

At one actual edge, a complete certificate must retain and independently
replay:

1. the two finite common-source groups and their reduction, including
   literal kernel generators \(s_i\);
2. every prefix-corrected local quotient \(H_{b,n}\), its reduction, and
   the complete common-source marginal maps;
3. the downstairs solution \(a_n\), canonical lift \(\sigma_n(a_n)\), and
   the unreduced residual \(d_n(a_n)\);
4. the invariant closure in (3.2), followed by either ancestry (3.5) or the
   complete dual in (3.4);
5. direct reconstruction of (2.4) in all tagged blocks and all side rows;
   and
6. destructive controls for one kernel word, prefix, return sign, quotient
   class, ancestry coefficient, and dual pairing.

The theorem solves only the linear common-source endpoint/marginal lift.
The actual word must additionally pass the nonlinear H1/H2/P depth
recurrence of v117/v260.  Prime-to-three formation and perfect-core accepted
sets also remain separate.
