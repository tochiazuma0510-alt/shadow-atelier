# R07 finite-quotient orbit obstruction selector v200

Author: Sol / 2026-08-28

Status: paper theorem refining the negative branch of v195--v198.  A finite
quotient of the exact PB endpoint groups cannot certify a zero exact
endpoint, but it can certify that no same-successor repair exists.  In the
finite quotient the full translating orbit is finite, so Gaussian
elimination returns a complete projected YES/NO answer and a projected dual
on NO.  The theorem also gives a small-normal-relator alternative to
enumerating the full Schreier basis.  No actual endpoint, compatible lift,
fake certificate, or Ihara witness is declared.

## 1. Exact repair problem

Put \(k=\mathbf F_3\), \(F=F(x,y)\), and let

\[
 F\xrightarrow{\psi_1}\Delta_1
\tag{1.1}
\]

be the actual finite first-successor map.  Write

\[
 J_1=\ker\bigl(k[F]\longrightarrow k[\Delta_1]\bigr).
\tag{1.2}
\]

For the three printed relation blocks put

\[
 E=k[PB_3]_{H1}\oplus k[PB_3]_{H2}\oplus k[PB_4]_P.
\tag{1.3}
\]

Let \(\eta(M_0)\in E\) be v194's three combined endpoints for one
word-bearing representative \(M_0\) of the first multiplier, and let

\[
 \mathcal E_d:k[F]\longrightarrow E
\tag{1.4}
\]

be the linear endpoint-change map.  By v195, an exact same-successor repair
exists precisely when

\[
 \eta(M_0)\in\mathcal E_d(J_1).
\tag{1.5}
\]

All eleven literal occurrences, including their signs, prefixes, inverse
slots, types, and printed-order combination, are part of (1.4).

## 2. Admissible finite endpoint quotients

Choose finite groups \(Q_{H1},Q_{H2},Q_P\) and marked quotient maps

\[
 \tau_{H1}:PB_3\twoheadrightarrow Q_{H1},\qquad
 \tau_{H2}:PB_3\twoheadrightarrow Q_{H2},\qquad
 \tau_P:PB_4\twoheadrightarrow Q_P.
\tag{2.1}
\]

They induce the linear map

\[
 \tau_*:E\longrightarrow
 E_Q:=k[Q_{H1}]\oplus k[Q_{H2}]\oplus k[Q_P].
\tag{2.2}
\]

Define

\[
 \bar\eta=\tau_*\eta(M_0),
 \qquad
 \bar{\mathcal E}_d=\tau_*\mathcal E_d.
\tag{2.3}
\]

The quotient is *admissible* when the producer and an independent checker
replay every marked generator image and all defining relators of (2.1).
No injectivity is assumed.  The two PB3 block copies may use the same finite
group, but remain separately tagged summands of \(E_Q\).

For each literal occurrence \(o\), let \(\rho_o:F\to PB_{3/4}\) be its
v194 substitution.  The joint finite action group is

\[
 D_Q=
 \operatorname{im}\!\left(
 F\longrightarrow\prod_{o=1}^{11}Q_{B(o)},\quad
 A\longmapsto(\tau_{B(o)}\rho_o(A))_o
 \right).
\tag{2.4}
\]

It is finite and is generated by the two exact marked tuples attached to
\(x\) and \(y\).  Repeated E3 values are retained in their two literal
positions; the E3 and E4 coordinates both labelled C21 are not identified.

## 3. Complete finite projected selector

Let \(h_1,\ldots,h_r\) be the complete Schreier basis of
\(\ker\psi_1\) from v196.  For \(d\in D_Q\), choose any source word
\(A_d\) with joint image \(d\), and put

\[
 C_{d,j}=\bar{\mathcal E}_d\bigl(A_d(h_j-1)\bigr)\in E_Q.
\tag{3.1}
\]

The value in (3.1) is independent of the chosen word \(A_d\), because the
endpoint-only formula v198 depends on \(A\) after (2.2) only through the
eleven-tuple (2.4).

### Theorem 3.1 (FINITE PROJECTED ORBIT COMPLETENESS)

The complete projected repair space is

\[
 \boxed{
 \bar{\mathcal E}_d(J_1)
 =\operatorname{span}_k
   \{C_{d,j}:d\in D_Q,\ 1\le j\le r\}.}
\tag{3.2}
\]

Consequently membership of \(\bar\eta\) in
\(\bar{\mathcal E}_d(J_1)\) is a finite exact linear-algebra decision.

#### Proof

V196 gives

\[
 J_1=\sum_{j=1}^r k[F](h_j-1).
\tag{3.3}
\]

Applying the linear map \(\bar{\mathcal E}_d\), every image is a finite
linear combination of columns
\(\bar{\mathcal E}_d(A(h_j-1))\).  Such a column equals (3.1) for the joint
image \(d\) of \(A\), proving one inclusion in (3.2).  Conversely every
element in the displayed roster is the image of an element of \(J_1\), so
the other inclusion follows.  Both \(D_Q\) and the Schreier roster are
finite. \(\square\)

An exhaustive BFS of the marked generators \(x^{\pm1},y^{\pm1}\) in the
finite product (2.4), followed by sparse echelon insertion, therefore has a
genuine completion terminal.  Unlike v196's exact PB orbit dovetail, a
completed projected failure is not a radius-bounded failure.

## 4. Small normal-relator alternative

Suppose a finite roster \(S=\{s_1,\ldots,s_t\}\subset F\) normally
generates \(\ker\psi_1\).  V195 gives the exact two-sided form

\[
 J_1=
 \sum_{i=1}^t k[F](s_i-1)k[F].
\tag{4.1}
\]

For \(d,e\in D_Q\), choose source representatives \(A_d,B_e\) and set

\[
 C'_{d,i,e}=
 \bar{\mathcal E}_d\bigl(A_d(s_i-1)B_e\bigr).
\tag{4.2}
\]

### Theorem 4.1 (FINITE TWO-SIDED NORMAL-RELATOR FORM)

\[
 \boxed{
 \bar{\mathcal E}_d(J_1)=
 \operatorname{span}_k
 \{C'_{d,i,e}:d,e\in D_Q,\ 1\le i\le t\}.}
\tag{4.3}
\]

#### Proof

Apply \(\bar{\mathcal E}_d\) to (4.1).  In every occurrence, the value of
\(A(s_i-1)B\) after projection is

\[
 \bar\rho_o(A)
 (\bar\rho_o(s_i)-1)
 \bar\rho_o(B),
\tag{4.4}
\]

so the full combined endpoint depends on \(A,B\) only through their two
joint images in \(D_Q\).  This gives (4.3). \(\square\)

Formula (4.3) may be much cheaper than (3.2) when a compact complete
normal-relator roster is already available but
\(r=|\Delta_1|+1\) is enormous.  It reintroduces two orbit parameters only
inside the finite screen.  The producer should estimate

\[
 |D_Q|r
 \quad\hbox{versus}\quad
 |D_Q|^2t
\tag{4.5}
\]

and use the smaller authenticated presentation.  Rank saturation may reduce
work, but it is not by itself a completion proof unless all remaining
columns are known to lie in the saturated span.

## 5. A projected NO is an exact NO

### Theorem 5.1 (QUOTIENT-DUAL OBSTRUCTION)

If

\[
 \boxed{
 \bar\eta\notin\bar{\mathcal E}_d(J_1),}
\tag{5.1}
\]

then

\[
 \boxed{
 \eta(M_0)\notin\mathcal E_d(J_1).}
\tag{5.2}
\]

Thus no finite-support representative of the same \(\mu_1\) has zero exact
three-block endpoint.

#### Proof

If an exact repair \(N\in J_1\) existed, then
\(\eta(M_0)=\mathcal E_d(N)\).  Applying \(\tau_*\) would give
\(\bar\eta=\bar{\mathcal E}_d(N)\), contradicting (5.1). \(\square\)

Because \(E_Q\) is finite-dimensional, (5.1) is equivalently certified by
a dual functional \(\lambda_Q:E_Q\to k\) such that

\[
 \lambda_Q(\bar\eta)\ne0,
 \qquad
 \lambda_Q(C_{d,j})=0
 \quad\text{for all }d,j,
\tag{5.3}
\]

or by the analogous full roster (4.2).  The pullback
\(\lambda_Q\tau_*\) is then an exact dual annihilator of every repair
column in the original PB group algebras.  This is a mathematical negative
certificate, not merely a failed search.

The converse is forbidden.  Projected membership

\[
 \bar\eta\in\bar{\mathcal E}_d(J_1)
\tag{5.4}
\]

does not imply exact membership.  Quotient collection may cancel distinct
PB words.  A projected coefficient solution is only a seed; it must be
lifted and replayed with the full Artin/Garside PB normal form of v198.

## 6. Refinement monotonicity and parallel schedule

Suppose an admissible finite endpoint quotient \(Q'\) maps onto another one
\(Q\), compatibly with all marked occurrence maps.  Then

\[
 \bar\eta_{Q'}\in\bar{\mathcal E}_{d,Q'}(J_1)
 \Longrightarrow
 \bar\eta_Q\in\bar{\mathcal E}_{d,Q}(J_1).
\tag{6.1}
\]

Hence a NO at any coarse level is terminal for every finer quotient and for
the exact endpoint problem.  A YES can be passed upward only as a candidate.

This permits a safe parallel schedule after the actual \(M_0\) is compiled.

1. Run the exact v198 endpoint test for \(M_0\).  If it is zero, skip every
   repair screen.
2. On a nonzero endpoint, launch several small independently authenticated
   finite endpoint quotients.  Each job enumerates its complete \(D_Q\),
   uses (3.2) or (4.3), and emits either a coefficient seed or the complete
   dual (5.3).
3. The first authenticated projected NO ends the same-\(\mu_1\) repair lane.
4. Projected YES seeds are replayed in exact PB normal form.  An exact pass
   proceeds to v197; an exact failure is refined or returned to v196's fair
   positive dovetail.

Small abelian, nilpotent, permutation, or already registered finite roof
images may all serve as screens, but only after their actual PB quotient maps
and occurrence typing are proved.  A hash, an unproved action image, or a
finite image with the E3/E4 C21 types merged is inadmissible.

## 7. Scope at the witness frontier

Theorems 3.1--5.1 close a genuine gap in v196's negative branch: a complete
finite projected orbit can prove that the canonical first multiplier has no
repair within its same-successor fibre.  They do not prove that another
first-successor multiplier is impossible, do not turn projected YES into an
exact repair, and do not discharge the later prime-to-three or perfect-core
gates.

The receipt binds the exact quotient presentations, all marked generator
images, the eleven typed occurrence maps, the complete joint group order and
BFS, the chosen Schreier or normal-relator roster, every generated column,
the final rank, and the dual pairing (5.3).  An independent checker rebuilds
the quotient groups and columns without importing producer helpers.
