# R07 Schreier lift-kernel endpoint homotopy (v310)

Author: Sol / 2026-08-29

Status: paper theorem and exact target for the return-even relative
homotopy.  V309 leaves an infinite family of literal representatives with
the same first-shadow coefficient.  This note proves that their differences
form a group-algebra kernel generated, as a left module, by a finite literal
Schreier roster.  Universal-endpoint repair is therefore the orbit of
finitely many explicit seed columns.  This does not prove that the actual
endpoint lies in that orbit and does not declare a lift or witness.

## 1. The finite-index lift kernel

Let \(\Gamma\) be v175's common source image in the product of the fixed
presented PB3/PB4 context groups, and retain the surjections

\[
 \Gamma\mathrel{\mathop{\twoheadrightarrow}^{\pi_1}}\Delta_1
 \mathrel{\mathop{\twoheadrightarrow}^{\pi_{10}}}\Delta_0.
\tag{1.1}
\]

Put

\[
 N_1=\ker\pi_1,
 \qquad
 N_0=\ker(\pi_{10}\pi_1),
\tag{1.2}
\]

so \(N_1\leq N_0\).  The group \(\Gamma\) is generated by the common source
images of \(x,y\), and \(\Delta_1\) is finite.

Choose a literal transversal

\[
 s:\Delta_1\longrightarrow\Gamma,
 \qquad s(1)=1,
\tag{1.3}
\]

for example the frozen first BFS words in \(x^{\pm1},y^{\pm1}\).  For
\(q\in\Delta_1\) and
\(t\in\{x,x^{-1},y,y^{-1}\}\), define the literal Schreier word

\[
 n(q,t)=s(q)t,s(q\bar t)^{-1}\in N_1,
\tag{1.4}
\]

where \(\bar t=\pi_1(t)\).  Delete identities and duplicates only after
retaining their literal word proofs, and write the resulting finite roster
as

\[
 \mathcal N_1=\{n_1,\ldots,n_s\},
 \qquad s\leq4|\Delta_1|.
\tag{1.5}
\]

Schreier rewriting proves that \(\mathcal N_1\) generates \(N_1\) as a
group.  No presentation of the infinite group \(\Gamma\) has to be guessed.

## 2. The group-algebra kernel is finitely orbit-generated

Put

\[
 J_0=\ker\bigl(k[\Gamma]\to k[\Delta_0]\bigr),
 \qquad
 \mathcal L_1=\ker\bigl(J_0\to
      I=\ker(k[\Delta_1]\to k[\Delta_0])\bigr),
\tag{2.1}
\]

with \(k=\mathbf F_3\).

### Lemma 2.1 (LIFT-KERNEL IDENTIFICATION)

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal L_1=ker(k[\Gamma]\to k[\Delta_1]).}
\tag{2.2}
\]

#### Proof

The right side lies in \(J_0\) because the map to \(\Delta_0\) factors
through \(\Delta_1\), so it is contained in the left side.  Conversely an
element of the left side has zero image in \(k[\Delta_1]\) by definition.
\(\square\)

### Theorem 2.2 (FINITE SCHREIER ORBIT GENERATION)

As a left \(k[\Gamma]\)-module,

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal L_1=sum_{i=1}^s k[\Gamma](n_i-1).}
\tag{2.3}
\]

Every finite-support element of \(\mathcal L_1\) has a finite expression

\[
 \ell=\sum_{j=1}^r a_jV_j(n_{i_j}-1),
 \qquad a_j\in k,quad V_j\in\Gamma.
\tag{2.4}
\]

Each summand is the literal roof-fibre pair

\[
 a_j(V_jn_{i_j}-V_j),
\tag{2.5}
\]

and is already trivial at \(\Delta_1\).

#### Proof

The kernel of a group-algebra quotient by \(N_1\) is the ideal generated by
\(n-1\), \(n\in N_1\).  It remains to prove that the finite group-generating
roster (1.5) suffices on the left.

If \(n=t_1\cdots t_r\) is a word in the \(n_i^{\pm1}\), then

\[
 n-1=(t_1-1)+t_1(t_2-1)+\cdots+
       t_1\cdots t_{r-1}(t_r-1),
\tag{2.6}
\]

and

\[
 n_i^{-1}-1=-n_i^{-1}(n_i-1).
\tag{2.7}
\]

Thus every \(n-1\) lies in the left module on the right of (2.3).
Conversely each \(n_i-1\) maps to zero in \(k[\Delta_1]\), proving equality.
Every group-algebra element has finite support, so expanding its finitely
many coefficients gives (2.4).  Since \(n_i\in N_1\leq N_0\), the two words
in (2.5) have equal \(\Delta_1\), hence equal roof, values.  \(\square\)

The finite roster (1.5) is a seed roster, not a finite endpoint column
roster: the translating elements \(V_j\) still range through \(\Gamma\).

## 3. The exact universal-endpoint repair module

Retain v193's occurrence-resolved universal rows
\(\widetilde d,\widetilde e\) and define the equivariant map

\[
 m_d:\mathcal L_1\longrightarrow\widetilde C_0,
 \qquad
 m_d(\ell)=\widetilde D_1(\ell\widetilde d)
                 =\ell\,\widetilde D_1\widetilde d.
\tag{3.1}
\]

The equality uses the diagonal left action in all seven separately tagged
blocks.  Put

\[
 \mathcal R_d=\operatorname{im}m_d.
\tag{3.2}
\]

### Corollary 3.1 (FINITE-SEED ENDPOINT ORBIT)

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal R_d=sum_{i=1}^s k[\Gamma]\,zeta_i,
 \qquad
 \zeta_i=\widetilde D_1((n_i-1)\widetilde d).}
\tag{3.3}
\]

Every positive ancestry in (3.3) compiles directly to the literal pairs
(2.5).

#### Proof

The map \(m_d\) is left \(k[\Gamma]\)-linear.  Apply it to (2.3), and use
(2.4)--(2.5) for the ancestry statement.  \(\square\)

Now fix one first-shadow multiplier \(\mu_1\) and one literal roof-fibre
lift \(M_{\rm can}\in J_0\).  Define its exact universal endpoint

\[
 \eta_{\rm can}=
 \widetilde D_1(\widetilde e-M_{\rm can}\widetilde d).
\tag{3.4}
\]

### Theorem 3.2 (EXACT REPRESENTATIVE-REPAIR CRITERION)

There exists another finite-support roof-fibre lift \(M\) of the same
\(\mu_1\) with universal endpoint zero if and only if

\[
 \boxed{\eta_{\rm can}\in\mathcal R_d.}
\tag{3.5}
\]

If

\[
 \eta_{\rm can}=m_d(\ell),
 \qquad \ell\in\mathcal L_1,
\tag{3.6}
\]

then

\[
 \boxed{M=M_{\rm can}+\ell}
\tag{3.7}
\]

is such a lift.  A coefficient ancestry in (3.3) makes (3.7) a finite
literal word-pair polynomial.

#### Proof

Every lift of the same first-shadow value is uniquely of the form
\(M_{\rm can}+\ell\) for some \(\ell\in\mathcal L_1\).  Its endpoint is

\[
 \widetilde D_1(\widetilde e-(M_{\rm can}+\ell)\widetilde d)
 =\eta_{\rm can}-m_d(\ell).
\tag{3.8}
\]

This vanishes exactly under (3.5)--(3.6).  The final assertion is Theorem
2.2.  \(\square\)

Combining Theorem 3.2 with v193 and v191 immediately promotes a positive
representative to every relative pro-3 rung, subject to v174's side gates.

## 4. This is the class-specific second homotopy

The map which the return-even calculation must split is now explicit:

\[
 \boxed{
 m_d:\mathcal L_1\longrightarrow\mathcal R_d,
 \qquad \ell\longmapsto\ell\widetilde D_1\widetilde d.}
\tag{4.1}
\]

It is not an abstract full \(H^2\) module and it is not the dihedral
antisymmetrizer itself.  It is the relative lift-kernel action on the actual
seven-block endpoint row.

Suppose the return involution preserves \(\mathcal L_1\), \(\mathcal R_d\)
and \(m_d\).  Since the characteristic is three, write

\[
 e_-=(1-\vartheta)/2,
 \qquad e_+=(1+\vartheta)/2.
\tag{4.2}
\]

On the return-odd part, the already typed relative-dihedral formula may give
a right inverse \(h_-\).  For the single actual return-even endpoint
\(e_+\eta_{\rm can}\), no right inverse on the whole even module is needed:
it is enough to find one literal

\[
 \ell_+\in e_+\mathcal L_1,
 \qquad
 m_d(\ell_+)=e_+\eta_{\rm can}.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Then

\[
 \ell=h_-(e_-\eta_{\rm can})+\ell_+
\tag{4.4}
\]

solves (3.6).  This is the endpoint analogue of v174's pointed Neumann
distinction: an annihilator-compatible linear section is needed only if one
wants a homotopy on the whole even orbit module, not to repair the one actual
endpoint.

Accordingly the concrete relative-dihedral generalization has the form

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{return-odd dihedral right inverse of }m_d
 \quad\oplus\quad
 \text{one return-even actual-class preimage under }m_d.}
\tag{4.5}
\]

This identifies exactly what an actual-class computation must produce.

## 5. Positive computation and honest negative boundary

The witness-first computation uses only finite seeds:

1. build \(\Delta_1\), the literal BFS transversal and every Schreier word
   (1.4);
2. independently replay that each word maps to one at \(\Delta_1\) and at
   the roof;
3. construct every seven-block seed \(\zeta_i\) in (3.3);
4. split the target and seeds by return sign when that action is
   authenticated;
5. close translated seed columns lazily under the common source generators,
   retaining literal translating words; and
6. stop on a finite equality (3.6), compile (3.7), and replay its zero
   universal endpoint.

If a finite-support solution exists, fair traversal of the translated
Schreier seeds eventually visits its finite support and exact column
generation terminates positively.  The acting group and its group algebras
are infinite, so a bounded no-hit is UNKNOWN unless a separate complete
module presentation or separating functional on the full orbit is proved.

A positive independent checker needs only the finite Schreier and translation
ancestry actually used.  It reconstructs the seven context normal forms,
every \(\zeta_i\), equation (3.6), the literal polynomial (3.7), endpoint
zero and v193's relator-decomposition chain.

V308 decides the fixed-rung A0/A5 state without losing the finite task193
affine prefixes.  V309 augments a fixed literal A0 candidate by the universal

\[
 \eta_{\rm can}\in
 \sum_{i=1}^s k[\Gamma]\zeta_i.
\tag{6.1}
\]

Thus no new arbitrary first-shadow multiplier is selected.  The finite

not allow all v308 state-equivalent ancestries to be merged unless their
universal endpoint normal forms agree.
