## 2. Class-specific contraction theorem

### Theorem 2.1 (ACTUAL-CLASS NEUMANN HOMOTOPY)

Assume there is a continuous \(\Lambda\)-linear map

\[
 s:L\longrightarrow A
\tag{2.1}
\]

such that

\[
 B s(L)\subseteq L,
 \qquad
 T:=1_L-Bs\ \text{satisfies}\ T(L)\subseteq JL.
\tag{2.2}
\]

Then the series

\[
 \boxed{
 h=s\sum_{m\geq0}T^m:L\longrightarrow A}
\tag{2.3}
\]

converges continuously and satisfies

\[
 \boxed{Bh=1_L.}
\tag{2.4}
\]

In particular the actual correction

\[
 \boxed{c_\infty=-h(\beta)}
\tag{2.5}
\]

solves \(B(c_\infty)=-\beta\) simultaneously at every finite reduction of
this lane.

#### Proof

Because \(T\) is \(\Lambda\)-linear, (2.2) implies inductively

\[
 T^m(L)\subseteq J^mL.
\tag{2.6}
\]

Completeness and separatedness therefore give convergence of (2.3).  Since
\(Bs=1_L-T\), its finite partial sums telescope:

\[
 Bs\sum_{m=0}^{N}T^m
 =(1_L-T)\sum_{m=0}^{N}T^m
 =1_L-T^{N+1}.
\tag{2.7}
\]

The last term tends to zero by (2.6), proving (2.4), and (2.5) follows.
\(\square\)

No commutativity of \(\Lambda\), determinant, Smith form, or contraction of
the ambient module \(Z\) is used.  This is the precise improvement over
demanding HT3 on every abstract residual class.

### Corollary 2.2 (finite reductions are automatically compatible)

Let \(h_n,s_n,T_n,L_n\) be the reductions of (2.3)--(2.4).  Then

\[
 h_n=s_n\sum_{m=0}^{n-1}T_n^m
\tag{2.8}
\]

up to any quotient killed by \(J^n\), and every finer correction reduces to
the coarser one.  Thus no independent choice of \(c_n\) is made.

#### Proof

All maps in (2.1)--(2.3) are one completed map and reduction commutes with
finite sums.  Terms in \(J^nL\) vanish at the displayed quotient. \(\square\)

## 3. What a first explicit lift does and does not prove

Suppose a finite computation returns

\[
 c_0\in A/JA,
 \qquad B_0c_0=-\beta_0.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Equation (3.1) is necessary evidence for (2.1), but it is not by itself a
map \(s_0:L/JL\to A/JA\).  Three additional facts are load-bearing:

1. the orbit relations presenting \(L/JL\) must be written literally;
2. the proposed images of its generators must satisfy those same relations
   in \(A/JA\); and
3. lifted word templates must make \(1-Bs\) preserve \(L\) and land in
   \(JL\), as in (2.2).

Once these hold, the first correction is not merely an isolated success: it
is the constant term of (2.3), and the higher terms

\[
 -sT\beta, -sT^2\beta,\ldots
\tag{3.2}
\]

are its forced successive refinements.

Conversely, a vector solution of (3.1) with no well-defined section can die
at the next rung.  This is why the current task186 search cannot be promoted
to an all-rung theorem merely from `COMMON_WORD`.

### Corollary 3.1 (one-dimensional base-class test)

Assume \(L/JL=\mathbf F_p\beta_0\).  A proposed \(c_0\) defines the base
section \(s_0(\beta_0)=-c_0\) exactly when every displayed stabilizer or
annihilator relation of \(\beta_0\) also kills \(c_0\) modulo \(JA\).
After choosing literal lifts, the remaining all-rung test is precisely
(2.2).

This gives a finite falsifier for the field-outer return-even line: compare
