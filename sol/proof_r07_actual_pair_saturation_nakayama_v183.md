# R07 actual-pair saturation and relative Nakayama promotion v183

Author: Sol / 2026-08-28

Status: paper theorem and a smaller all-rung promotion gate. This note
replaces the whole-subsystem successor-kernel hypothesis of v182 by a
condition on the two-generator module spanned by the original defect and one
corrected residual. The condition has an exact finite coinvariant-dimension

## 1. One relative \(3\)-edge

Let \(k=\mathbf F_3\), let

\[
 \pi:G'\twoheadrightarrow G,
 \qquad K=\ker\pi
\tag{1.1}
\]

be a surjection of finite groups whose kernel is a \(3\)-group, and put

\[
 \Lambda'=kG',\qquad \Lambda=kG,
 \qquad I=\ker(\Lambda'\to\Lambda).
\tag{1.2}
\]

Let \(Z'\) be a finite left \(\Lambda'\)-module whose registered reduction
is

\[
 Z=Z'/IZ'.
\tag{1.3}
\]

Choose compatible elements \(d',e'\in Z'\), with reductions \(d,e\in Z\),
and define only the actual-pair modules

\[
 M'=\Lambda'd'+\Lambda'e'\subseteq Z',
 \qquad
 M=\Lambda d+\Lambda e\subseteq Z.
\tag{1.4}
\]

The natural map \(M'/IM'\to M\) is always onto. Its kernel is

\[
 \boxed{
 \ker(M'/IM'\to M)
   ={M'\cap IZ'\over IM'}.}
\tag{1.5}
\]

Thus the exact condition needed below is the pair-saturation identity

\[
 \boxed{M'\cap IZ'=IM'.}
\tag{1.6}
\]

Unlike v182 (1.4), this makes no assertion about every direction in an
ambient actual-defect subsystem. It controls only the module generated by
the two named compatible rows \(d'\) and \(e'\).

## 2. Nilpotence of the relative group-algebra ideal

### Lemma 2.1 (RELATIVE \(3\)-KERNEL IDEAL IS NILPOTENT)

The ideal \(I\) in (1.2) is nilpotent.

#### Proof

Let \(J_K\subseteq kK\) be the augmentation ideal. Since
\(K\triangleleft G'\), conjugation by \(G'\) preserves \(J_K\). Hence

\[
 I=\Lambda'J_K=J_K\Lambda'
\tag{2.1}
\]

and induction gives \(I^r=\Lambda'J_K^r\). The augmentation ideal of the
group algebra of a finite \(3\)-group over \(\mathbf F_3\) is nilpotent.
Therefore \(J_K^N=0\), and hence \(I^N=0\), for some \(N\). \(\square\)

No commutativity of \(G'\), \(K\), or \(\Lambda'\) is used.

## 3. The two-generator promotion theorem

### Theorem 3.1 (ACTUAL-PAIR SATURATION--NAKAYAMA PROMOTION)

Assume (1.3), (1.6), and

\[
 e\in\Lambda d.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{e'\in\Lambda'd'.}
\tag{3.2}
\]

Moreover, every specified downstairs coefficient lifts: for each
\(\mu\in\Lambda\) satisfying \(e=\mu d\), there is a
\(\mu'\in\Lambda'\) satisfying

\[
 \boxed{
 e'=\mu'd',\qquad \pi(\mu')=\mu.}
\tag{3.3}
\]

#### Proof

By (3.1), \(M=\Lambda d\). Equations (1.5)--(1.6) therefore say that the
image of \(d'\) generates \(M'/IM'\). Equivalently,

\[
 M'=\Lambda'd'+IM'.
\tag{3.4}
\]

Put \(Q=M'/\Lambda'd'\). Equation (3.4) gives \(Q=IQ\). Lemma 2.1 gives
\(I^N=0\), so

\[
 Q=IQ=I^2Q=\cdots=I^NQ=0.
\tag{3.5}
\]

Consequently \(M'=\Lambda'd'\), and in particular (3.2) holds.

Now fix a solution \(\mu\) in (3.3) downstairs and choose any lift
\(\widetilde\mu\in\Lambda'\). Then

\[
 z=e'-\widetilde\mu d'
 \in M'\cap IZ'=IM'.
\tag{3.6}
\]

The first part gives \(M'=\Lambda'd'\), hence \(IM'=Id'\). Choose
\(\kappa\in I\) with \(z=\kappa d'\). Then
\(\mu'=\widetilde\mu+\kappa\) has the required two properties.
\(\square\)

This is a relative Nakayama argument, but (3.5) proves it directly from
nilpotence and remains valid for noncommutative group algebras.

## 4. Exact dimension and freeness certificates

### Proposition 4.1 (PAIR-COINVARIANT DIMENSION CRITERION)

Under (1.3), pair saturation (1.6) is equivalent to

\[
 \boxed{
 \dim_k(M'/J_KM')=\dim_k M.}
\tag{4.1}
\]

If \(K=\langle s_1,\ldots,s_t\rangle\), then

\[
 J_KM'=\sum_{i=1}^t(s_i-1)M'.
\tag{4.2}
\]

Thus (4.1) requires only the orbit span of the two actual rows and the action
matrices of a generating set of \(K\).

#### Proof

The map \(M'/IM'\to M\) from Section 1 is a surjection of finite
\(k\)-spaces, and its kernel is (1.5). Normality of \(K\) gives
\(IM'=J_KM'\). Therefore its kernel is zero exactly when the source and
target dimensions agree. Formula (4.2) is v180 Lemma 2.1. \(\square\)

This is an exact necessary-and-sufficient finite test. It is smaller than
constructing the whole intersection in (1.6), and smaller still than a
freeness test on the ambient quotient below.

Put

\[
 Q'=Z'/M'.
\tag{4.3}
\]

### Proposition 4.2 (RELATIVE-FREE QUOTIENT CERTIFICATE)

If the restriction of \(Q'\) to \(kK\) is projective (equivalently, for
finite \(K\), free), then (1.6) holds.

#### Proof

Restrict

\[
 0\longrightarrow M'\longrightarrow Z'\longrightarrow Q'
 \longrightarrow0
\tag{4.4}
\]

to \(kK\). Projectivity of \(Q'\) splits (4.4) as \(kK\)-modules, so write
\(Z'=M'\oplus Q'\). Acting by \(J_K\) gives

\[
 J_KZ'=J_KM'\oplus J_KQ'.
\tag{4.5}
\]

Intersecting with \(M'\) yields

\[
 M'\cap J_KZ'=J_KM'.
\tag{4.6}
\]

Because \(IZ'=J_KZ'\) and \(IM'=J_KM'\), this is (1.6). \(\square\)

Equivalently, the obstruction to (1.6) is the image of

\[
 \operatorname{Tor}^{kK}_1(k,Q')
 \longrightarrow M'/J_KM'.
\tag{4.7}
\]

Thus full freeness is sufficient, not logically necessary. For a complete
finite module, v179--v180 give the generator-only certificate

\[
 \boxed{
 \dim_k Q'=|K|\dim_k(Q'/J_KQ').}
\tag{4.8}
\]

The right-hand coinvariant space requires only the action matrices of a
registered generating set of \(K\), not an enumeration of all elements of
\(K\).

## 5. All-rung form and the pointed Neumann multiplier

Let

\[
 \Lambda_{n+1}\twoheadrightarrow\Lambda_n,
 \qquad Z_{n+1}/I_nZ_{n+1}\cong Z_n
\tag{5.1}
\]

be the diagonal-context system on a matched relative pro-\(3\) tower. Let
\(d=(d_n)\) be the compatible original signed target and let
\(e=(e_n)\) be the compatible residual obtained by evaluating one fixed
literal first correction word at every rung. Set

\[
 M_n=\Lambda_nd_n+\Lambda_ne_n.
\tag{5.2}
\]

### Corollary 5.1 (PAIR-SATURATED COFINAL COEFFICIENT SELECTOR)

Suppose \(e_0=0\) and, at every successor, either pair saturation

\[
 M_{n+1}\cap I_nZ_{n+1}=I_nM_{n+1}
\tag{5.3}
\]

or the exact dimension certificate of Proposition 4.1 holds. The sufficient
relative-freeness certificate of Proposition 4.2 is a stronger alternative.
Then

\[
 e_n=\mu_nd_n
\tag{5.4}
\]

for a compatible family of coefficients \(\mu_n\). The family can be
chosen with \(\mu_0=0\), so its inverse-limit value lies in the relative
augmentation ideal \(\mathfrak j\).

#### Proof

Start with the solution \(\mu_0=0\). Apply Theorem 3.1 recursively. Its
last assertion says that the complete solution fibre at rung \(n+1\) maps
onto the chosen solution at rung \(n\), so the coefficients can be chosen
compatibly. Reduction to the roof is zero, which is exactly membership in
\(\mathfrak j\). \(\square\)

Under the word-bearing and nonlinear hypotheses of v174, the resulting
coefficient \(\mu=(\mu_n)\) gives the explicit compatible correction

\[
 \boxed{
 c_\infty=-\sum_{r\ge0}\mu^r a.}
\tag{5.5}
\]

Thus a roof success propagates through every relative pro-\(3\) rung without
separately choosing an unrelated correction at each rung, provided the
two-generator pair is saturated at every edge.

## 6. Exact R07 finite certificate

At one genuine successor the smaller certificate is:

1. the complete block-tagged boundary quotient \(Z'\) and its diagonal
   \(K\)-generator actions;
2. the actual literal rows \(d'\) and \(e'\), with direct seven-context Fox
   replay;
3. the complete diagonal translates spanning
   \(M'=\Lambda'd'+\Lambda'e'\);
4. the exact pair-coinvariant equality (4.1), with the matrices \(s_i-1\)
   and direct reduction replay; alternatively, a direct equality
   \(M'\cap IZ'=IM'\), or the stronger quotient-freeness equality (4.8);
5. a lifted coefficient \(\mu'\) and direct replay of \(e'=\mu'd'\), or a
   complete failure certificate for the registered saturation/freeness
   condition.

The expensive boundary correlation can use the exact shard/merge theorem
v176 and task194's implementation, but all pivots and quotient actions are
formed only after the global coefficientwise merge. A resource stop is
UNKNOWN; failure of freeness does not prove failure of saturation or of the
actual multiplier.

This gate is strictly smaller than v182's demand
\(\ker(D_{n+1}\to D_n)=I_nd_{n+1}\) for an entire actual-defect subsystem.
V183 only asks that the two named rows do not acquire a new relation when
embedded through one relative edge. The remaining all-rung obligation is
still structural: prove (5.3) uniformly from the universal seven-context
presentation, or establish (4.1) at every cofinal edge.
