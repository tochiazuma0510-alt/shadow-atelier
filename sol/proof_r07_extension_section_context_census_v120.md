## 2. Quotient-section formula

Choose an arbitrary set-theoretic section

\[
 s:Q_0\longrightarrow G,
 \qquad \rho s=1,
 \tag{2.1}
\]

with \(s(1)=1\).  Define

\[
 L_S=\{q\in Q_0:\varphi_S(s(q))\in A_S\}.
\tag{2.2}
\]

### Theorem 2.1 (EXTENSION-SECTION IMAGE CENSUS)

The set \(L_S\) is independent of the choice of the section in (2.1), is a
normal subgroup of \(Q_0\), and there is an exact sequence

\[
 \boxed{
 1\longrightarrow A_S\longrightarrow D_S
 \longrightarrow Q_0/L_S\longrightarrow1.}
\tag{2.3}
\]

In particular,

\[
 \boxed{|D_S|=|A_S|\,[Q_0:L_S].}
\tag{2.4}
\]

#### Proof

Because \(\Gamma\triangleleft G\), its image \(A_S\) is normal in \(D_S\).
The composite

\[
 G\xrightarrow{\varphi_S}D_S\longrightarrow D_S/A_S
\tag{2.5}
\]

kills \(\Gamma\), hence factors through a surjective homomorphism

\[
 \overline\varphi_S:Q_0\longrightarrow D_S/A_S.
\tag{2.6}
\]

For any lift \(g\) of \(q\), equation (2.6) sends \(q\) to the coset
\(\varphi_S(g)A_S\).  Replacing \(g\) by another lift multiplies it by an
element of \(\Gamma\), so the coset is unchanged.  Consequently (2.2) is
exactly \(\ker\overline\varphi_S\).  It is therefore section-independent
and normal.  The first isomorphism theorem gives (2.3), and (2.4) follows.
\(\square\)

This theorem replaces a BFS in \(D_S\) by two smaller exact operations:
enumerate the 243 images in \(A_S\), then scan the already finite \(Q_0\)
sections and test (2.2).

## 3. Projection kernels and fibres

For \(S\subseteq T\subseteq\{1,2,3\}\), coordinate deletion gives a
surjection

\[
 p_{T,S}:D_T\twoheadrightarrow D_S.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Hence

\[
 \boxed{
 |\ker p_{T,S}|=
 \frac{|A_T|[Q_0:L_T]}{|A_S|[Q_0:L_S]}.}
\tag{3.2}
\]

Every nonempty fibre of \(p_{T,S}\) has this size.  In particular, for the
three coordinate maps used in v118,

\[
 |\ker\pi_i|=|D_{123}|/|D_i|.
\tag{3.3}
\]

The common kernel of all three literal coordinate maps on
\(D_{123}\leq E_4^3\) is the identity tuple.  This is a typing fact, not an
experimental invariant:

\[
 \ker\pi_1\cap\ker\pi_2\cap\ker\pi_3=1.
\tag{3.4}
\]

To obtain word-bearing kernel generators rather than only orders, put

\[
 H_i=\ker(\varphi_i:G\to D_i),
 \qquad \Gamma_i^0=\Gamma\cap H_i.
\tag{3.5}
\]

Restriction of \(\rho\) gives another exact sequence

\[
 \boxed{
 1\longrightarrow\Gamma_i^0\longrightarrow H_i
 \xrightarrow{\rho}L_i\longrightarrow1.}
\tag{3.6}
\]

Indeed, the image is contained in \(L_i\).  Conversely, if \(q\in L_i\),
choose \(\gamma_q\in\Gamma\) with

\[
 \varphi_i(\gamma_q)=\varphi_i(s(q))^{-1}.
\tag{3.7}
\]

Then \(\gamma_qs(q)\in H_i\) maps to \(q\).  The kernel in (3.6) is
immediate.  Therefore a generating set of \(\Gamma_i^0\), together with the
words \(\gamma_qs(q)\) for a generating set of \(L_i\), maps to a
word-bearing generating set of \(\ker\pi_i\) in \(D_{123}\).  This is the
kernel representation required by v118 even when the kernel is too large to
list element by element.

## 4. Deterministic executable form

An exact implementation may use the following bounded route for each of the
seven nonempty subsets \(S\subseteq\{1,2,3\}\).

1. Project all 243 authenticated Gamma states to the exact ordered E4
   coordinates in \(S\).  Deduplicate exact blobs to obtain \(A_S\), its
   order, multiplication table or generators, and canonical digest.
2. Reconstruct all 1,469,664 `Q0=P x G9` states and deterministic source
   section words from the pinned factor presentations and split words.
3. Evaluate \(\varphi_S(s(q))\) and test exact membership in \(A_S\).
   Record precisely the states in \(L_S\); check identity, multiplication,
   inverses, normality under the two marked generators, and a canonical
   membership digest.
4. Apply (2.4) and (3.2).  Cross-check every divisibility and the equality of
   direct and iterated projection orders.
5. Retain one Gamma section solving (3.7) and a source word for each selected
   generator of \(L_i\).  Directly replay every resulting kernel generator
   in all three contexts.

The seven scans may share the evaluation of one `Q0` section, but their
ordered tuple keys and membership tables remain separately typed.  Hash
equality alone is not group-element equality.

If the task-174 direct BFS closes under its registered two-million-state cap,
its exact state set must agree with (2.4) and supplies a stronger direct
cross-check.  If that BFS reaches its cap, the bounded miss remains
`UNKNOWN_RESOURCE` for the direct route; it does not invalidate the
extension-section route.  The latter needs its own independent checker and
must not be inferred merely from the integer in (1.2).
