## Producer certificate

For a positive A4 input, the producer authenticates both receipt and
independent verdict, all three A4 milestones, queue exhaustion, complete
ordered `K_roster`, literal word ancestry, all four marked action matrices,
inverse laws, and the exact task176/task198 roof square.

It composes each canonical residual word in the frozen multiplication order,
then directly binds that order for every residual generator and every K basis
word by replaying both

```text
s u s^-1
[s,u] = s u s^-1 u^-1.
```

Every block column retains its full ordered K-coordinate vector, literal-word
and fine-value digests, coarse identity and integer exponent sums.  Every
rank-raising column additionally retains the literal commutator word, full
direct fine value, literal ancestry, separately labelled v37 formation
ancestry, normalization, earliest pivot, reduction trace and full
change-of-basis row.  All block columns and the final span coordinates are
exported.  No extra side gate is intersected.

## Independent checker

The checker:

- restores the frozen v14 checker rather than the v12 producer arithmetic;
- scans Gamma states, record generators, center states and central pairs in
  reverse orders and uses a different preliminary inner representative;
- evaluates word actions by applying marked matrices from the rightmost
  letter, instead of using the producer's matrix-composition routine;
- builds the block span in generator/K reverse order with latest pivots;
- checks span containment in both directions against the producer's
  earliest-pivot basis;
- replays every producer block, every selected literal commutator, every
  ancestry, normalization and pivot; and
- directly compares both independently constructed canonical generator
  values, not their possibly different source-word spellings.

The producer module is never imported and no new helper is shared.

## Driver contract

`D382Dispatch` defaults to `false`; the parsed driver prints
`R07_A4_LEGAL_SOURCE_EXTRACTOR_V1_UNDISPATCHED` and performs no dispatch.

On a later explicit dispatch:

- no A4 pin variables means task176-only v360 materialization, with matching
  producer/checker `UNKNOWN_INPUT` terminals and both sealed artifacts kept;
- all six `D382A4ReceiptPath/Bytes/SHA` and
  `D382A4VerdictPath/Bytes/SHA` variables mean the full A4 extraction path;
- a partial A4 pin tuple is rejected;
- stale receipt, verdict, logs, terminal files, shell and sentinel are
  rejected;
- producer/checker progress is exposed through `tee`, both artifacts and logs
  remain in `ci/out`, and exactly one matching terminal line is required.

No checkpoint machinery was added: the only new group work is bounded by
243 states, 729 central pairs, five short relators and a 504-state complement
closure, followed by finite F3 row reduction.
