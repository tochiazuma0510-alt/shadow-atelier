#### Proof

\[
 \eta\theta_j(L_{s,P})

## 3. Conjugator cancellation in the Fox derivative

Use the left Fox-pair convention of v467,

\[
 \Phi(w)=(\eta(w),D(w)),\qquad
 (g,u)\odot(h,v)=(gh,u+gv).                         \tag{3.1}
\]

### Lemma 3.1 (one literal conjugate)

If (eta(r)=1), then for every (P\in F),

\[
 D(PrP^{-1})=\eta(P)D(r),\qquad
 D(Pr^{-1}P^{-1})=-\eta(P)D(r).                    \tag{3.2}
\]

#### Proof

The product and inverse rules give

\[
\begin{aligned}
 D(PrP^{-1})
 &=D(P)+\eta(P)D(r)+\eta(P)\eta(r)D(P^{-1})\\
 &=D(P)+\eta(P)D(r)-D(P).
\end{aligned}
\]

This is the first identity.  Since (D(r^{-1})=-D(r)) at endpoint one, the
second follows identically. \(\square\)

In particular, the derivative of the actor word itself is never needed for
the selected correction.  Its marked endpoint is needed, and is computed
with the exact v443 affine/truncated normal-form arithmetic separately in
each occurrence.
