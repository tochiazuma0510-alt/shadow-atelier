## 2. The actual eleven-context ledger

Use freely reduced signed integer words and the conventions

\[
 x=[1],\quad y=[2],\quad
 \operatorname{PP}(w_1,\ldots,w_k)=\operatorname{red}(w_k\cdots w_1),
\]

\[
 z=\operatorname{PP}(x,y)^{-1}=[-1,-2],\qquad
 u=\operatorname{PP}(y,x)^{-1}=[-2,-1].
\]

registered PB3 lift

\(\iota_3\) exactly for ordinals 1--6.  Let \(\eta_j\) be the correctly

\[

\[

## 3. Exact source recurrence and the three roots

The accepted Task625 payload must preserve two different objects:

1. the canonical noncommutative constructor DAG and its roots; and
2. a derived exact leaf coefficient map.

The DAG is the authority.  For every reachable node \(v\), maintain an exact
map

\[
 A_v:P\longmapsto a_{v,P}\in\mathbf F_3
\]

on freely reduced tuples over `(-2,-1,1,2)`.  Initialize the 3,317 registered
grade pivots, in Task595 order and with their coefficients, at path
\(P=\varepsilon\).  A nonactor reduction/scalar edge keeps the same exact
path and multiplies its coefficient by the registered scalar.  An actor edge
with registered pure word \(q\) sends

\[
 (P,a)\longmapsto (\operatorname{red}(Pq),a\,c_e).     \tag{3.1}
\]

Thus actor words are appended on the right using the exact conventional
left-to-right word product; boundary inverse letters cancel.  Contributions
are added modulo three, and zero may be deleted only after equality of the
full `(node, exact_tuple_path)` key.  Graph reachability is separately
traversed over every registered edge, including edges whose coefficients
later cancel.  Endpoint, signature, hash or seed equality is not allowed in
this recurrence.

At the literal layer, exact equal keys give the derived map
\(\mu_T(s,P)\).  It represents the selected update root

```text
C_T = OrderedProduct(3317 GradeNodeRef powers in exact Task595 order).
```

The prior root is separately reconstructed from
`prepare.canonical_solution.terms` in its existing order:

```text
C_<1 = RegisteredPriorProduct(exact listed terms).
```

It must not be sorted, normalized into a different product, or inferred from
the Task625 leaf stream, which contains only the selected `C_T` traversal.
Apply the same exact leaf derivation to obtain \(\mu_{<1}(s,P)\).  Finally

```text
C_1 = Compose(C_<1,C_T)
```

means prior followed by update, with that exact source syntax.  Only after
all eleven endpoint-one gates and all three roots have been authenticated may
the Fox identity

\[
 D(C_1)=D(C_{<1})+D(C_T)
\]

be used and equal exact keys added to form
\(\mu_1=\mu_{<1}+\mu_T\).  This additive evaluation is not an equality or
commutation of the source words.

## 4. Exact endpoint trie and the only permitted coalescence

Construct one prefix trie from all nonzero exact paths in \(\mu_1\), while
retaining terminal pointers back to every exact `(s,P)` key.  For each typed
occurrence, initialize

\[
 E_j(\varepsilon)=1
\]

and, for every trie edge labelled by a signed letter \(\ell\), use

\[
 E_j(P\ell)=E_j(P)\,\eta_j\theta_j(\ell).              \tag{4.1}
\]

The multiplication is on the right and proceeds in source-word order.
Negative-letter images are exact group inverses.  The complete signature is
the ordered, typed tuple

\[
 \Sigma_{11}(P)=(E_1(P),\ldots,E_{11}(P))
 \in E3^6\times E4^5.                                  \tag{4.2}
\]

An internal ten-coordinate cache is permitted only if it materializes the
two coordinate-0 occurrences separately before hashing or serializing
(4.2).  A six-entry signature presented as an eleven-entry one, `% 6`, an
unordered set, or an untyped endpoint byte string is a hard failure.

Before any signature grouping, directly check

\[
 \eta_j\theta_j(r_s)=1                                 \tag{4.3}
\]

for every reached seed and all eleven occurrences.  There are at most
\(44\cdot11=484\) such typed gates.  The exact graph, exact paths,
path-to-signature table and all gate results are sealed first.  Only then is
the current-quotient evaluation table

\[
 \bar\mu(s,\tau)=
 \sum_{P:\Sigma_{11}(P)=\tau}\mu_1(s,P)\pmod3         \tag{4.4}
\]

formed; zero buckets may be omitted, but different seeds may never be
combined.  Let

```text
L = number of nonzero exact (s,P) keys in mu_1
U = number of distinct exact path prefixes, including epsilon
G = number of nonzero (s,Sigma_11) buckets.
```

Then `G <= L`.  The receipt binds all path/signature assignments, all
nonzero buckets and `L/U/G`; counts alone are insufficient.  The checker must
independently evaluate every path or use an independently written recurrence
(4.1), for example an equivalent suffix trie with its multiplication order
proved against (4.1).

Equality in (4.2) licenses only regrouping the already authenticated finite
Fox sum at the present quotients.  It does **not** identify source words,
remove DAG edges, prove equality in a refinement, or allow cancellation
before (4.3).  This supplies the required answer to the endpoint/source-word
question: source preservation is a premise of endpoint coalescence, not an
optimization which endpoint coalescence can replace.

## 5. Eleven authentication slots versus the six-tag physical row

The proposed hypothesis is correct under the registered target/projection
premises; no extra adapter is needed.

- V12f ordinals 1--6 are exactly the six H substitutions
  `(x,y),(x,z),(y,z),(u,x),(x,y),(u,y)` used by Task565's `floor.OO`.
- Their signs are exactly Task565's aggregation coefficients
  `(1,2,1,2,2,1)` over \(\mathbf F_3\), and their two destination blocks are
  H1 for ordinals 1--3 and H2 for ordinals 4--6.
- Task565's fixed shifts
  `(1,g_yz,g_yz,g_uy*g_xy^-1,g_uy,g_uy)` are precisely
  \((U_1,\ldots,U_6)\).
- V445 (2.4), (4.2) and v446 (1.4), (5.2) define the operative occurrence
  source as six tags times two Fox components and the physical target as two
  hexagons times two PB3 components times 504 positions.  At degree two this
  gives
  `4*6*2*6*504 = 145152` source trits and
  `4*2*2*6*504 = 48384` physical trits.
- V437's projection statement drops the PB4 block; v441/v451 require that
  exact boundary quotient and occurrence-first aggregation to commute with
  the filtration.  The five E4/P slots therefore authenticate the complete
  all-seven source interpretation but do not supply coordinates to this
  projected H target.

Define the restriction, after the full eleven receipt has passed, by

\[
 \pi_H\Sigma_{11}(P)=(E_1(P),\ldots,E_6(P)).           \tag{5.1}
\]

This is typed coordinate selection, not an action map from an eleven-tag
module and not a quotient by ordinal modulo six.  The eleven-signature
partition refines the H-six partition.  Consequently evaluating every
nonzero eleven bucket with the first six components and summing gives exactly
the same H row as first merging buckets having equal \(\pi_H\tau\).  Keeping
the finer eleven buckets, as required here, is conservative and directly
implements v471's theorem.

There is an important counterexample boundary.  Two exact paths may have the
same six H endpoints but different P endpoints.  They give the same current
two-hexagon Fox action, yet need not give the same full all-seven/P-retaining
row or the same endpoints in a refinement.  Thus P components are not proved
zero and are not discarded from the source/endpoint receipts.  They are only
outside the codomain of this particular physical projection.  A future
P-retaining/B4 consumer needs a new registered operator and dimensions; it
cannot reuse v451/v474 by aliasing five P slots into six H slots.

The restriction conclusion therefore requires all of the following explicit
premises:

1. the target is exactly the v451/v446 two-hexagon precision-two target, not
   v12f's unprojected direct all-seven object;
2. the accepted PB4-drop/boundary quotient and its filtration-commutation
   receipt are pinned and independently replayed;
3. Task565's six substitution, sign and prefix tables are compared entrywise
   with ordinals 1--6 above; and
4. the implementation never describes P derivatives as zero or uses the H
   residual as a full all-seven residual.

Absent any one premise, the safe result is `NOT_READY`, not an inferred
`11 -> 6` adapter.
