## 2. Multisorted contraction theorem

### Theorem 2.1 (FILTERED ACTUAL-CLASS CONTRACTION)

Suppose there is a continuous additive map

\[
 s:L\longrightarrow A
\tag{2.1}
\]

such that \(Bs(L)\subseteq L\).  Put

\[
 T=1_L-Bs.
\tag{2.2}
\]

If

\[
 \boxed{
 T(\mathcal F^mL)\subseteq\mathcal F^{m+1}L
 \quad\text{for every }m\geq0,}
\tag{2.3}
\]

then

\[
 \boxed{
 h=s\sum_{j\geq0}T^j}
\tag{2.4}
\]

is a continuous additive map \(L\to A\) and

\[
 \boxed{Bh=1_L.}
\tag{2.5}
\]

Consequently, for the compatible actual defect \(\beta\in L\),

\[
 \boxed{c_\infty=-h(\beta)}
\tag{2.6}
\]

is one correction at all finite reductions of this fixed-prime lane.

#### Proof

Equation (2.3) gives

\[
 T^j(\mathcal F^mL)\subseteq\mathcal F^{m+j}L.
\tag{2.7}
\]

Thus the partial sums in (2.4) are Cauchy after applying the continuous map
\(s\), and completeness of \(A\) gives their limit.  Since \(Bs=1_L-T\),

\[
 Bs\sum_{j=0}^{N}T^j=1_L-T^{N+1}.
\tag{2.8}
\]

Separatedness and (2.7) make the last term tend to zero.  This proves
(2.5), and (2.6) follows. \(\square\)

The proof uses neither commutativity nor a common PB3/PB4 deck algebra.
What it does use is the full quantified filtration condition (2.3).

## 3. When a first-layer test is enough

The shorter hypothesis of v171,

\[
 T(L)\subseteq JL,
\tag{3.1}
\]

implies (2.3) only after a common action has actually been registered.
Precisely, suppose a complete filtered ring \((\Xi,J)\) acts continuously on
\(A\) and on every block of \(L\), all structural maps and \(B,s\) are
\(\Xi\)-linear, and

\[
 \mathcal F^mL=J^mL.
\tag{3.2}
\]

Then

\[
 T(J^mL)=J^mT(L)\subseteq J^{m+1}L,
\tag{3.3}
\]

so the single first-layer inclusion (3.1) proves all of (2.3).

This is an application gate, not a notational convention.  Equal
dimensions, matching return signs, or separate PB3/PB4 group-ring actions
do not construct \(\Xi\).

### Proposition 3.1 (FINITE GENERATOR CANARY)

Under the registered common-action hypotheses above, let
\(\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_t\) topologically generate \(L\) as a
\(\Xi\)-module.  Then

\[
 \boxed{
 T(L)\subseteq JL
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 T(\ell_i)\in JL\quad(1\leq i\leq t).}
\tag{3.4}
\]

#### Proof

The forward implication is immediate.  Conversely, \(\Xi\)-linearity gives
\(T(\sum_i a_i\ell_i)=\sum_i a_iT(\ell_i)\in JL\) on the algebraic span.
Continuity and closedness of \(JL\) give the result on its completion.
\(\square\)

In the genuinely cyclic case \(L=\overline{\Xi\beta}\), one actual
second-rung canary \(T(\beta)\in JL\) is therefore sufficient.  If the
block-tagged orbit needs several generators, every generator in (3.4) must
be tested.  If no common \(\Xi\)-action has been authenticated, even all
first-layer generator tests do not replace (2.3).

## 4. Relation condition for a word-bearing section

Let a finite free cover of the actual module be

\[
 q:\Xi^t\twoheadrightarrow L,\qquad q(e_i)=\ell_i.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Choose word-bearing values \(a_i\in A\).  The formula

\[
 s\!\left(\sum_i\lambda_i\ell_i\right)
   =\sum_i\lambda_i a_i
\tag{4.2}
\]

defines the map required in Theorem 2.1 exactly when

\[
 \boxed{\ker q\subseteq
 \ker\bigl(\Xi^t\to A,\ e_i\mapsto a_i\bigr).}
\tag{4.3}
\]

For \(t=1\), (4.3) is the v111 annihilator condition

\[
 \operatorname{Ann}_{\Xi}(\beta)\,a_1=0.
\tag{4.4}
\]

Thus a task186 vector which kills the displayed defect is not yet \(s\).
It becomes the constant term of the all-rung formula only after the literal
orbit relations (4.3) and either (2.3) or the valid reduction
(3.1)--(3.3) have been proved.

## 5. Exact role of task193

A positive task193 artifact has two distinct uses.

1. It types the actual next residual in the genuine block-tagged successor,
   excluding a Jennings surrogate.
2. It supplies the first nontrivial comparison transcript needed to identify
   the error \(T(\ell_i)\) for the registered generators.

It does not by itself provide:

* the common action \(\Xi\);
* the complete relation presentation (4.3);
* the correction images \(a_i\);
* \(\Xi\)-linearity of the word template; or
* the filtration-raising statement beyond the materialized classes.

The shortest promotion certificate after task186/task193 is therefore:

1. construct the block-tagged actual orbit roster
   \(\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_t\) and all its relations;
2. give literal correction words \(a_i\) and prove (4.3);
3. replay \(B(a_i)\) and the task193 defects to prove (3.4);
4. authenticate the common action and (3.2)--(3.3), or instead prove the
   ring-free condition (2.3) directly; and
5. apply (2.4), followed by the word and side-gate replay.

This separates a finite second-rung canary from the missing uniformity
theorem without demanding contraction of the ambient residual module.

## 6. Mixed primes and perfect cores

Theorem 2.1 applies to one fixed-prime complete lane.  In the mixed-prime
solvable ladder, use the corresponding filtered contraction on each lane
and the authenticated triangular formation maps to interleave the
corrections.  A nonabelian perfect-core edge is not an additive filtration
step; its finite accepted-set nonemptiness remains a separate gate.
