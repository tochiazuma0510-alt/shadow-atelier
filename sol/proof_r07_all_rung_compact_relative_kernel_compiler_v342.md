# R07 all-rung compact relative-kernel compiler (v342)

Author: Sol / 2026-08-29

Status: paper theorem combining the complete projected-kernel split of
v340, the compact generators of v341, and the edge-local compatibility
theorem v259.  At every split elementary-abelian refinement, the complete
affine correction image can be computed from a finite primitive roster;
one never enumerates the full prefix-collision basis.  A MEMBER ancestry
produces a literal correction in the accumulated coarse kernel, so different
edgewise Schreier trees and sections need not be compatible.  The theorem
does not prove MEMBER at every edge, does not replace the nonabelian gates,
and declares no compatible R07 lift, fake certificate, or Ihara witness.
`verified=false`.

## 1. One actual refinement edge

Let `k=F_p`, let `F=F(x,y)`, and retain one accumulated coarse/fine edge

\[
 F\twoheadrightarrow G\mathrel{\mathop{\twoheadrightarrow}^{\pi}}G_0,
 \qquad K=\ker\pi.
\tag{1.1}
\]

Let the projected relative-dihedral quotient be

\[
 q:G\twoheadrightarrow D,
 \qquad R=q(K),
 \qquad \bar q:G_0\twoheadrightarrow D/R.
\tag{1.2}
\]

Assume that `K` is elementary abelian and that `q|_K` has a group section

\[
 \sigma:R\longrightarrow K.
\tag{1.3}
\]

Put

\[
 K_0=K\cap\ker q,
 \quad
 I=\ker(k[G]\to k[G_0]),
 \quad
 I_R=\ker(k[D]\to k[D/R]),
\tag{1.4}
\]

and write `Q=q_*|_I`.  Choose a word-bearing linear section

\[
 \alpha:I_R\longrightarrow I,
 \qquad Q\alpha=1.
\tag{1.5}
\]

Let the projected endpoint factor as

\[
 \Phi:I\longrightarrow E,
 \qquad \Phi=\Psi Q,
\tag{1.6}
\]

where `Q`, `Phi`, and `Psi` are equivariant for the indicated quotient
actions.  The chosen section `alpha` is only linear and is not assumed
equivariant.
Suppose a projected solution `v0 in I_R` satisfies

\[
 \Psi(v_0)=\bar\epsilon.
\tag{1.7}
\]

Then v340 gives the complete affine slice

\[
 \Phi^{-1}(\bar\epsilon)
 =\alpha(v_0)+T,
 \qquad
 T=\alpha(\ker\Psi)+\ker Q.
\tag{1.8}
\]

Because `T=ker Phi`, it is a left `k[G]`-submodule even though the chosen
linear section `alpha` need not be equivariant.

## 2. Uniform primitive roster

Choose:

1. a basis `b_1,...,b_u` of `ker Psi`, with word-bearing lifts
   `a_i=alpha(b_i)`;
2. an elementary-abelian basis `ell_1,...,ell_t` of `K0`, with retained
   source words; and
3. the prefix-closed Reidemeister--Schreier basis `h_1,...,h_s` of

   \[
    H=\ker(F\longrightarrow D/R).
   \tag{2.1}
   \]

Since `F` has rank two,

\[
 \boxed{s=|D/R|+1.}
\tag{2.2}
\]

If `g_j` is the value of `h_j` in `G`, set

\[
 r_j=q(g_j)\in R,
 \qquad
 n_j=g_j\sigma(r_j)^{-1}.
\tag{2.3}
\]

Let `B_R={r-1:r in R, r != 1}` be the standard basis of the augmentation
ideal of `k[R]`, and let `j:k[R]->k[K]` be induced by `sigma`.  Define

\[
 c_{j,r}=(n_j-1)(\sigma(r)-1)
 \qquad(1\le j\le s,\ r\in R\setminus\{1\}).
\tag{2.4}
\]

Each (2.4) expands into the four word-bearing terms

\[
 n_j\sigma(r)-n_j-\sigma(r)+1.
\tag{2.5}
\]

### Theorem 2.1 (ALL-EDGE COMPACT TANGENT GENERATORS)

Under (1.1)--(1.7),

\[
 \boxed{
 T=k[G]\left\langle
     a_i,\ \ell_l-1,\ c_{j,r}:
     1\le i\le u,\ 1\le l\le t,\
     1\le j\le |D/R|+1,\ r\ne1
   \right\rangle.}
\tag{2.6}
\]

Thus the complete affine tangent is generated by

\[
 \boxed{u+t+(|D/R|+1)(|R|-1)}
\tag{2.7}
\]

primitive word-bearing seed types.

#### Proof

The `a_i` span `alpha(ker Psi)`.  V341 Theorem 3.2 proves

\[
 \ker Q=k[G]\langle\ell_l-1,c_{j,r}\rangle.
\tag{2.8}
\]

Combining (1.8) and (2.8) proves that the right side of (2.6) contains `T`.
Conversely every displayed seed lies in `T`: this is immediate for `a_i`,
the local differences lie in `ker Q`, and v341 Lemma 3.1 puts every
collision seed in `ker Q`.  Equivariance makes `T=ker Phi` stable under
left `k[G]`, so the whole right side lies in `T`.  This proves equality.
\(\square\)

The count (2.7) is a generator count, not a dimension formula and not an
assertion that the image columns are independent.

## 3. Complete finite MEMBER compiler

Let

\[
 \mathscr D:T\longrightarrow Z
\tag{3.1}
\]

be any finite equivariant pointed/pentagon/side-coordinate correction map.
For the affine base `mu_base=alpha(v0)`, put

\[
 z_{\rm req}=z_{\rm target}-\mathscr D(\mu_{\rm base}).
\tag{3.2}
\]

Start with the images under \(\mathscr D\) of the primitive roster (2.6),
and repeatedly
apply the marked generator actions `x^{+/-1},y^{+/-1}`.  Retain a column only
when it raises the current rank, together with its parent, action letter and
literal source ancestry.  Since `Z` is finite-dimensional, this queue
terminates.

### Theorem 3.1 (EXACT COMPACT-CLOSURE MEMBER TEST)

When the queue is exhausted, its span is exactly \(\mathscr D(T)\).
Consequently:

1. `z_req` is MEMBER exactly when elimination against the retained basis has
   zero remainder;
2. on MEMBER, the ancestry reconstructs a literal coefficient
   `tau in T` and hence `mu=mu_base+tau` in the complete affine slice; and
3. on NONMEMBER, a dual row annihilating the exhausted closure but pairing
   nontrivially with `z_req` separates the entire legal tangent, not merely a
   bounded word-radius prefix.

#### Proof

Theorem 2.1 says every element of `T` is a finite linear combination of left
translates of the primitive seeds.  Equivariance sends those translates to
the corresponding marked actions in `Z`.  Hence the invariant closure of
the seed images is \(\mathscr D(T)\).  Rank-raising insertion terminates
after at most
`dim Z` insertions and does not change the spanned subspace.  Ordinary finite
linear algebra gives the three conclusions.  \(\square\)

This is an exact alternative to enumerating either all elements of `G0` or
the vector-space basis of the prefix-collision summand.

## 5. From one edge to one compatible profinite thread

At level `n`, include every already frozen evaluation in the accumulated
coarse quotient `G0`.  Every word-bearing difference in (2.6) then has equal
coarse endpoints.  Materializing a MEMBER ancestry therefore returns a
correction word

\[
 c_n\in N_n,
\tag{5.1}
\]

where `N_n` is the accumulated evaluation kernel.  V259 applies even if the
Schreier tree, group section, row basis and normalized ancestry are chosen
afresh at the next edge.

### Corollary 5.1 (CONDITIONAL ALL-RUNG EXPLICIT COMPILER)

Suppose every active elementary-abelian edge satisfies the split hypothesis
(1.3), and Theorem 3.1 returns MEMBER for the recursively reached actual
target at every such edge.  Suppose also that every intervening nonabelian
accepted set is nonempty and all side gates pass.  Choosing the normalized
first MEMBER ancestry and the shortlex-first nonabelian correction at each
edge produces

\[
 f_{n+1}=f_nc_n,
 \qquad c_n\in N_n,
\tag{5.2}
\]

and the product `f0*c0*c1*...` is one explicit compatible profinite word.

#### Proof

Theorem 3.1 gives each abelian correction with complete ancestry; the stated
finite rule chooses each nonabelian correction.  Equation (5.1) is exactly
the premise of v259 Theorem 2.1, which gives compatibility and convergence.
\(\square\)

This closes the **construction and compatibility** problem after stagewise
success.  It does not infer later success from the first MEMBER result.

## 6. The remaining uniform-success alternatives

There are three distinct sufficient routes from the finite compiler to all
edges.

1. **Structural homotopy.**  Prove the complete score pairing of the compact
   legal roster is onto on the nonlinear-stable residual module.  V334 then
   supplies one Newton/Neumann right inverse used at every depth.
2. **No-new-overlap propagation.**  Prove v332's overlap spaces descend at
   every refinement.  One initial MEMBER then propagates through the linear
   tower; the literal/nonlinear hypotheses still require replay.
3. **Actual-class recurrence.**  Prove only that the recursively reached
   residual class lies in the compact closure at every edge.  Corollary 5.1
   then constructs one based lift without a module-wide right inverse.

The third route is logically weakest and is the witness-first route.  A
single first-edge MEMBER is not by itself any of these three statements.
The role of v341--v342 is to make each required full-domain test finite and
complete without silently dropping the field-even prefix-collision block.

## 7. Certificate boundary

For each elementary-abelian edge, an acceptable certificate records:

1. the exact accumulated coarse/fine groups and maps `pi`, `q`, and `bar q`;
2. the split A4 kernel basis, projected anchor and `K0` basis;
3. the exact `D/R` Cayley tree and complete Schreier roster;
4. all adjusted words `n_j` and direct `q(n_j)=1` checks;
5. all primitive projected, local and collision seed words;
6. the complete rank-exhausted action closure with ancestry;
7. a primal MEMBER equality or a full-closure dual NONMEMBER row; and
8. the accumulated-kernel check for the materialized correction.

Mutations of the section adjustment, multiplication side, one four-word
term, one action edge, one pivot, target coordinate or ancestry must be
rejected.  A bounded-radius queue or local-`K0`-only roster is incomplete.
