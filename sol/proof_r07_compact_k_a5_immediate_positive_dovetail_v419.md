## 1. Independence is not a positive-soundness premise

Retain v418's notation

\[
 R=\ker(F(x,y)\to\Delta _0),\quad
 K=\ker(\Delta _1\to\Delta _0),\quad
 I=\ker(\mathbf F_3[\Delta _1]\to\mathbf F_3[\Delta _0]).
 \tag{1.1}
\]

For any literal word \(u\in R\), put \(k=\rho _1(u)\). Then

\[
 k\in K,\qquad k-1\in I,\qquad
 g(k-1)\in I\quad(g\in\Delta _1).                         \tag{1.2}
\]

These containments use neither independence of \(k\) in K nor completion of
the K conjugation closure.

### Lemma 1.1 (LEGAL CANDIDATE COLUMN)

Every finite pre-C column

\[
 g\widehat\Psi(k-1)
 \quad\longleftarrow\quad
 (wu)-w,\qquad \rho _1(w)=g,                              \tag{1.3}
\]

is a legal A5/A6 coefficient column. If a finite raw reduction using such
columns and an exact PB-boundary ledger reaches the target, the resulting
A5 MEMBER and literal M are sound even if the K quotient status of some
source words has not yet been decided.

#### Proof

Equation (1.2) puts every coefficient in the true domain I. Linearity of the
pre-C map and the raw-to-quotient PB ledger give the A5 equality. The source
identity

\[
 \rho _1((wu)-w)=gk-g=g(k-1)                             \tag{1.4}
\]

gives the literal A6 ancestry. No spanning assertion is used. \(\square\)

## 2. The correct positive-first schedule

Run the following fair dovetail.

1. Evaluate all 44 Task411 compact relators directly in A5 and insert their
   pre-C columns immediately.
2. Send the same 44 literal words to the K quotient reducer.
3. Whenever an accepted K pivot spawns one of its four marked conjugates,
   evaluate that literal conjugate in A5 immediately, before or concurrently
   with its K quotient reduction.
4. Only a K-rank-raising candidate spawns four further K conjugates. A
   K-dependent candidate may leave a rank-raising A5 row, but needs no K
   ancestry node beyond its exact reduction ledger.
5. Interleave marked left-action closure of the accepted A5 columns and the
   two typed PB-boundary oracles. Stop at the first exact A5 target hit.

The A5 echelon itself retains only rank-raising rows, so immediate insertion
does not require retaining every dependent K candidate.

### Theorem 2.1 (EARLY POSITIVE / LATE NEGATIVE)

The schedule above has the following two properties.

1. Every finite MEMBER terminal is sound.
2. If no finite hit occurs, A5 NONMEMBER is permitted only after the K
   conjugation frontier, K quotient-boundary oracle, A5 left-action frontier,
   and A5 PB-boundary oracle all exhaust as in v418.

#### Proof

Part 1 is Lemma 1.1. For part 2, K exhaustion returns an ordinary
word-bearing basis by v418 Theorem 3.1; v418 Theorem 3.2 then identifies the
full left ideal I, and exhaustion of the A5 action and PB oracle identifies
the full image of the target map. Before those statements hold, the searched
span is only a legal subspace of the full image. \(\square\)
