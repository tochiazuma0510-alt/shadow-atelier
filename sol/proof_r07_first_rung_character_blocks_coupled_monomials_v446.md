## 1. Setting and the distinction that matters

Let

\[
 k=\mathbf F_3,\qquad P=PSL(2,8),\qquad A=C_2^2,
 \qquad V=C_3^3,
\]

and consider the split rung

\[
 1\longrightarrow V\longrightarrow Q_2
 \longrightarrow Q_1=P\times A\longrightarrow1.       \tag{1.1}
\]

In the section-left, kernel-right coordinates of v443, the degree-
\(d\) associated grade has monomial basis

\[
 \mathcal B_d=\{\alpha\in\{0,1,2\}^3:|\alpha|=d\},
 \qquad h_d=|\mathcal B_d|,                            \tag{1.2}
\]

and

\[
 I^d/I^{d+1}
   =\bigoplus_{\lambda\in\widehat A}
      k[P]e_\lambda\otimes k\mathcal B_d.             \tag{1.3}
\]

The occurrence source additionally has the fixed six tags and two Fox
components.  Thus a fixed-character degree-
\(d\) source block has width

\[
 6\cdot2\cdot |P|\,h_d=6048h_d.                       \tag{1.4}
\]

For \(d=1\), \(h_1=3\).  The four safe source blocks therefore have width
\(18144\) each and total width \(72576\).

The key distinction is between an invariant ambient direct sum and the
submodule generated by a particular set of defects.  Invariance of every
monomial summand does not by itself put the coordinate projection onto that
summand in the actor algebra.

## 2. The Fourier character splitting is generated by legal actors

For a marked actor \(\ell\), the associated-grade formula of v443 is

\[
 L_\ell([p,a]u^\alpha)
   =[p_\ell p,a_\ell+a]u^\alpha.                       \tag{2.1}
\]

The four marked actors and their inverses generate the marked quotient
\(Q_1\).  In particular, the explicit pure-sign complement words from v442
give the operators \(L_{(1,a)}\) for every \(a\in A\).  Hence the following
operators belong to the algebra generated by the legal source actors:

\[
 e_\lambda=\sum_{a\in A}\lambda(a)L_{(1,a)},
 \qquad \lambda\in\widehat A.                         \tag{2.2}
\]

Here \(|A|=4=1\) in \(k\), and all characters have order two, so (2.2) is
the usual normalized central idempotent.  The four operators are orthogonal
and sum to the identity.

Let \(D_d\) be the full set of seed and transition defects constructed by
v444, and let

\[
 H_d=k\langle Q_1\rangle D_d                           \tag{2.3}
\]

be their exhausted legal actor closure in the pure new grade.  Since every
\(e_\lambda\) is a legal linear combination of actor words,

\[
 \boxed{H_d=\bigoplus_{\lambda\in\widehat A}H_{d,\lambda},
 \qquad H_{d,\lambda}=e_\lambda H_d.}                 \tag{2.4}
\]

Thus one may project every *full* defect with the four character
idempotents and exhaust four independent character closures.  This is an
equality of generated submodules, not merely an ambient invariance claim.

## 3. Monomial projections are not legal in general

On a fixed character block, (2.1) acts on the regular \(P\)-coordinate and
by the character scalar, but it acts identically on the multiplicity space
\(k\mathcal B_d\).  Consequently the actor algebra need not contain the
coordinate projector

\[
 \operatorname{id}_{k[P]e_\lambda}\otimes
 \operatorname{pr}_\alpha.                            \tag{3.1}
\]

The elementary counterexample already occurs for an arbitrary nonzero
actor module \(R\).  In \(R\otimes k^2\), let every actor act as the same
operator on both coordinates and generate from

\[
 z=r\otimes(m_1+m_2).                                 \tag{3.2}
\]

Its orbit span is the diagonal copy

\[
 \{s\otimes(m_1+m_2):s\in k\langle Q_1\rangle r\},   \tag{3.3}
\]

which need not contain either monomial projection of \(z\).  Closing those
projections separately replaces (3.3) by a larger direct sum and can turn a
genuine NONMEMBER residual into a false MEMBER.

Therefore the v445 inference

\[
 H_{d,\lambda}
 \stackrel{?}{=}
 \bigoplus_{\alpha\in\mathcal B_d}
 \operatorname{pr}_{\lambda,\alpha}H_d               \tag{3.4}
\]

is withdrawn.  No certificate may use (3.4) unless it separately proves
that the required monomial projectors preserve the particular generated
submodule.  The first-rung implementation does not assume this.

## 4. Correct transition-defect algorithm

At precision \(d+1\), retain the complete lower exhausted basis and all four
actor transition records required by v444.  Then:

1. lift each seed relation and each retained transition through the exact
   v443 arithmetic;
2. perform its deterministic lower reduction, applying identical row
   operations to the lifted coordinates;
3. retain the resulting zero-lower pure-grade defect with its full monomial
   tuple and literal ancestry;
4. apply the four legal projectors (2.2) to that full defect;
5. in each character, exhaust the actor orbit in the complete
   \(6048h_d\)-coordinate space, never projecting individual monomials;
6. retain the coupled-monomial basis and its ancestry for the physical fibre
   construction.

By (2.4) and the transition-defect theorem v444, this computes exactly the
new-grade source space.  At degree one it replaces twelve unsafe width-6048
closures by four exact width-18144 closures.  It does not restart the old
orbit, enumerate the 54,432 group elements, or construct a multiplication
table.

## 5. Physical aggregation and the complete support graph

For a single homogeneous coordinate, the six occurrence maps still obey
the audited leading transport

\[
 (\lambda,\alpha)\longmapsto
 (\lambda\circ A_j^{-1},\pi_j\alpha)                 \tag{5.1}
\]

with the registered sign.  Formula (5.1) is useful for routing and checking
support, but it no longer proves that a *row* lies in one component of the
vertex graph: a legal source row in \(H_{d,\lambda}\) may contain several
monomials, whose images can occupy several such components.

The fail-closed physical construction is therefore:

1. lower-reduce every lifted old basis aggregate and retain every
   zero-lower grade remainder;
2. aggregate every basis row of every exact coupled-monomial
   \(H_{d,\lambda}\);
3. insert both families into one complete degree-
   \(d\) physical fibre echelon; and
4. reduce the actual residual only after this fibre is exhausted.

For degree one the safe joint physical width is

\[
 4\cdot |Q_1|\,h_1=24192.                              \tag{5.2}
\]

An implementation may recover smaller independent physical solves only
from the **actual-row hypergraph**: its vertices are the target
\((\lambda,\alpha)\) blocks, and every nonzero-support set of every
aggregated coupled defect row and every lifted-old connection row is added
as a hyperedge.  Connected components of that completed hypergraph give a
sound direct sum.  The occurrence permutation graph by itself is only a
support routing graph and is not sufficient.

The first executable should use the joint width (5.2) unless the completed
hypergraph is constructed and sealed in its certificate.
