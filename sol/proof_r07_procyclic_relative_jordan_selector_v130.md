# R07 procyclic relative Jordan selector v130

Author: Sol / 2026-08-27

Status: paper theorem.  It generalizes the fixed `C9/C3` calculation of
v70 to every index-p step in a cyclic p-power tower.  It gives a closed
rank test, identifies all bad indecomposable lengths, and gives an explicit
based preimage on permutation blocks.  Its R07 application is conditional
on a typed comparison between the actual normalized Brunnian obstruction
quotient and the displayed cyclic cohomology module.  It does not assert
that every actual R07 factor is permutation, does not solve a nonabelian
chief edge, and does not declare a fake or Ihara witness.

## 1. One arbitrary cyclic p-power edge

Let

\[
 k=\mathbf F_p,\qquad
 P=\langle g\rangle\cong C_q,\qquad q=p^a,
 \qquad H=\langle g^p\rangle\cong C_{q/p},
\tag{1.1}
\]

and let \(V\) be a finite-dimensional \(kP\)-module.  Put

\[
 J=g-1\in kP.
\tag{1.2}
\]

In characteristic \(p\),

\[
 J^q=0,\qquad
 N_P=1+g+\cdots+g^{q-1}=J^{q-1},
\tag{1.3}
\]

and

\[
 g^p-1=J^p,qquad
 N_H=1+g^p+\cdots+g^{q-p}=J^{q-p}.
\tag{1.4}
\]

The standard periodic cyclic resolution therefore gives

\[
 H^2(P,V)=\ker J/J^{q-1}V,
 \qquad
 H^2(H,V)=\ker J^p/J^{q-p}V.
\tag{1.5}
\]

Restriction sends the class of a \(P\)-invariant representative to the
class of the same vector.  Hence:

### Theorem 1.1 (PROCYCLIC RELATIVE KERNEL)

For every finite \(kP\)-module \(V\),

\[
 \boxed{
 \ker\!\left(H^2(P,V)\longrightarrow H^2(H,V)\right)
 =\frac{\ker J\cap J^{q-p}V}{J^{q-1}V}.}
\tag{1.6}
\]

#### Proof

The formulas (1.3)--(1.5) follow from
\((X-1)^q=X^q-1\) and

\[
 \frac{X^q-1}{X^p-1}=(X-1)^{q-p}
\]

over \(k\).  A class represented by \(v\in\ker J\) restricts to zero
precisely when \(v\in N_HV=J^{q-p}V\).  Quotienting by
\(N_PV=J^{q-1}V\) proves (1.6). \(\square\)

This is a relative detection theorem.  It says that once an arithmetic
restriction is zero, only the small quotient (1.6) can still carry the
global class.

## 2. Complete Jordan classification

Since

\[
 kP\cong k[J]/(J^q),
\tag{2.1}
\]

every finite \(kP\)-module is a direct sum of the uniserial blocks

\[
 J_\ell:=k[J]/(J^\ell),\qquad 1\leq\ell\leq q.
\tag{2.2}
\]

### Theorem 2.1 (ONLY THE TOP p-1 TRUNCATIONS ARE BAD)

For one indecomposable block,

\[
 \boxed{
 \ker\!\left(H^2(P,J_\ell)\longrightarrow H^2(H,J_\ell)\right)
 \cong
 \begin{cases}
 k,&q-p+1\leq\ell\leq q-1,\\
 0,&1\leq\ell\leq q-p\ \text{or}\ \ell=q.
 \end{cases}}
\tag{2.3}
\]

Thus exactly the \(p-1\) almost-regular truncations

\[
 J_{q-p+1},J_{q-p+2},\ldots,J_{q-1}
\tag{2.4}
\]

can carry a relative class.

#### Proof

On \(J_\ell\), the line \(\ker J\) is generated by the bottom vector
\(J^{\ell-1}\).  It lies in \(J^{q-p}J_\ell\) exactly when
\(\ell\geq q-p+1\).  The denominator \(J^{q-1}J_\ell\) is zero for
\(\ell<q\), while for \(\ell=q\) it is exactly \(\ker J\).  Applying
(1.6) gives (2.3). \(\square\)

For \(p=3\), the list (2.4) has only two entries:

\[
 \boxed{J_{3^a-2}\quad\text{and}\quad J_{3^a-1}.}
\tag{2.5}
\]

V70's \(J_7,J_8\) pair is the case \(a=2\).  At the next cyclic scale
the only possible pair is \(J_{25},J_{26}\), then
\(J_{79},J_{80}\), and so on.  There is no growing zoo of obstruction
types.

## 3. A basis-free rank certificate

Let

\[
 r_i=\operatorname{rank}(J^i:V\to V).
\tag{3.1}
\]

### Corollary 3.1 (THREE-RANK TEST)

One has

\[
 \boxed{
 \dim_k\ker\!\left(H^2(P,V)\to H^2(H,V)\right)
 =r_{q-p}-r_{q-p+1}-r_{q-1}.}
\tag{3.2}
\]

In particular, restriction is injective in degree two exactly when

\[
 \boxed{r_{q-p}-r_{q-p+1}=r_{q-1}.}
\tag{3.3}
\]

If \(\beta\in\ker J\) is known to restrict to zero, then its relative
class vanishes exactly when

\[
 J^{q-1}u=\beta
\tag{3.4}
\]

has a solution.  A returned \(u\) is an explicit positive certificate; an
inconsistent system together with a separating left-nullspace row is an
exact negative certificate for this typed module and class.

#### Proof

The numerator of (1.6) is the kernel of

\[
 J:J^{q-p}V\longrightarrow J^{q-p+1}V,
\]

so it has dimension \(r_{q-p}-r_{q-p+1}\).  Its subspace
\(J^{q-1}V\) has dimension \(r_{q-1}\).  This proves (3.2)--(3.3), and
(3.4) is precisely membership in the denominator of (1.6). \(\square\)

For \(p=3\), only the ranks at

\[
 3^a-3,\qquad3^a-2,\qquad3^a-1
\tag{3.5}
\]

are needed.  This is a uniform finite calculation at an arbitrary cyclic
scale; a full Jordan basis is unnecessary.

## 4. Permutation blocks are uniformly safe

Let \(\Omega\) be a finite \(P\)-set and let \(k[\Omega]\) be its
permutation module.  A transitive orbit has cardinality \(p^b\) for some
\(0\leq b\leq a\), and its permutation module is one block
\(J_{p^b}\).

### Theorem 4.1 (PERMUTATION-SAFE RELATIVE DETECTION)

Assume \(a\geq2\).  For every finite \(P\)-set \(\Omega\),

\[
 \boxed{
 H^2(P,k[\Omega])\longrightarrow H^2(H,k[\Omega])
 \quad\text{is injective}.}
\tag{4.1}
\]

Moreover, after choosing one basepoint on each full \(P\)-orbit, (3.4)
has the following closed based solution.  Write a restricted-zero invariant
as

\[
 \beta=\sum_{O\ {m full}}c_O\sum_{\omega\in O}\omega.
\tag{4.2}
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{u=\sum_{O\ {m full}}c_O\,\omega_O}
\tag{4.3}
\]

satisfies \(J^{q-1}u=\beta\).

#### Proof

Every non-full orbit has size at most \(q/p\).  For \(a\geq2\),

\[
 q/p\leq q-p,
\tag{4.4}
\]

so its Jordan length lies below the bad interval (2.4).  A full orbit is
the regular block \(J_q\), which is also safe by Theorem 2.1.  This proves
(4.1).

On a non-full orbit, \(J^{q-p}=0\), so it contributes nothing to a vector
in the numerator of (1.6).  On a full orbit,

\[
 J^{q-1}\omega_O=\sum_{\omega\in O}\omega.
\tag{4.5}
\]

Applying (4.5) to (4.3) proves the explicit formula. \(\square\)

At the first edge \(C_p\to1\), fixed-point permutation orbits can carry
cohomology, so the hypothesis \(a\geq2\) is necessary.  The R07 fixed
`C9/C3` edge and all higher cyclic 3-power edges lie in the safe range.

The selector (4.3) is deliberately based, not equivariant.  This is
compatible with the v114 modular norm-transfer warning: an equivariant
natural splitting is unnecessary for the one sequential branch of v129.

## 5. Typed use on the actual R07 class

Let \(\mathcal O_n\) be the normalized Brunnian obstruction quotient of
v72 at one abelian diagram-chief edge, and let \(o_n\in\mathcal O_n\) be
the actual class made by the already chosen R07 word.  Suppose a cyclic
roof segment gives a displayed, class-preserving identification

\[
 \boxed{
 (\mathcal O_n,o_n)\cong
 \left(
 \ker\bigl(H^2(C_{3^a},V_n)\to
 H^2(C_{3^{a-1}},V_n)\bigr),[\beta_n]
 \right).}
\tag{5.1}
\]

Require in addition that (5.1) is induced by a displayed cochain-level map:
a solution \(J^{3^a-1}u=\beta_n\) must transport to a word-bearing
degree-one preimage in v72 and hence to the \((d,\rho)\) coordinates of the
v129 cyclic-saturation equation.  This comparison is load-bearing.  Merely
finding a cyclic action somewhere in a larger coefficient module does not
imply (5.1), and a bare vector-space isomorphism of obstruction quotients
does not transport a correction.

### Corollary 5.1 (RETURN-EVEN ACTUAL-CLASS SELECTOR)

Under (5.1), any one of the following proves \(o_n=0\) and returns the
cyclic preimage needed by the v72/v129 correction:

1. \(V_n\) is a permutation \(C_{3^a}\)-module with \(a\geq2\);
2. the three-rank identity

   \[
   r_{3^a-3}-r_{3^a-2}=r_{3^a-1}
   \tag{5.2}
   \]

   holds; or
3. the one actual system \(J^{3^a-1}u=\beta_n\) is solved.

This applies without regard to return parity.  Thus the return-odd part may
still use the dihedral antisymmetrizer, while (5.1)--(5.2) provide a genuine
class-specific route for the field-outer return-even survivor.

#### Proof

Theorem 4.1 proves item 1, Corollary 3.1 proves item 2, and (1.6) proves
item 3.  The comparison (5.1) transports the resulting zero class and
preimage back to the actual normalized Brunnian quotient. \(\square\)

## 6. Cofinal consequence

Consider one fixed cofinal ladder and its diagram-chief refinement.  Assume:

1. the first exact all-seven word has been constructed;
2. every abelian edge admits the typed comparison (5.1);
3. at every such edge one of the three conditions of Corollary 5.1 holds;
4. the cochain-level comparison following (5.1) transports the preimage to
   an admissible word-bearing representative and to v129 coordinates; and
   and
5. every encountered nonabelian accepted set is nonempty.

### Theorem 6.1 (PROCYCLIC-DIHEDRAL INTERLEAVE CRITERION)

Under these hypotheses the v129 cyclic saturation class vanishes at every
abelian edge.  Applying the v129 formula sequentially, and interleaving the
chosen nonabelian corrections, produces a compatible inverse-limit R07
lift.

No independently natural family of preimage selectors is required: each
new word is constructed as a lift of the preceding word.  In particular,
if every actual cyclic comparison module is permutation, the infinitely
many abelian membership problems are discharged by the single symbolic
Theorem 4.1 rather than by infinitely many unrelated searches.

#### Proof

Corollary 5.1 kills the actual normalized obstruction at every abelian
edge.  The typed comparison supplies the corresponding cyclic-saturation
coordinates, so v129 Theorem 5.1 constructs the compatible next word.  At
a nonabelian edge use the retained accepted-set element.  Induction gives a
compatible family, and completeness and separatedness give its inverse
limit. \(\square\)

## 7. Exact new frontier

The all-stage abelian problem is now reduced to a concrete dichotomy at
each cyclic three-primary segment:

```text
permutation / no J_(3^a-2),J_(3^a-1):  automatic based correction;
bad near-regular block present:         solve the one actual class;
typed comparison (5.1) absent:          comparison remains UNKNOWN.
```

The next finite receipt after the first all-seven word should therefore
retain, on the actual normalized obstruction quotient:

1. the cyclic generator matrix \(J\);
2. the three ranks in (3.5);
3. the actual restricted-zero representative \(\beta\);
4. either its orbit/basepoint permutation certificate or a solution of
   (3.4); and
5. the explicit transport from that solution to the word-bearing

also stronger than one finite-stage success: the symbolic exclusion of the
