# R07 A0: graded Fourier blocks for the first C3^3 rung (v445)

Author: Sol / 2026-09-03

Status: candidate optimization theorem, conditional on v442--v444.  It
decomposes every pure new-grade actor closure for the split
(Q_2\to Q_1=PSL(2,8)\times C_2^2) extension into small exact blocks and
then gives a fail-closed construction of the joint physical block graph.  It changes no legal
source space and proves no residual membership. `verified=false`.

## 1. Associated grade of the split extension

Use the section-left, kernel-right coordinates of v443.  Put

\[
 P=PSL(2,8),\quad A=C_2^2,\quad V=C_3^3,
 \quad k=\mathbf F_3,
\]

and let (G_d=I^d/I^{d+1}) be a positive group-algebra grade for

\[
 1\to V\to Q_2\to Q_1=P\times A\to1.                 \tag{1.1}
\]

If

\[
 \mathcal B_d=\{\alpha=(\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3):
 0\leq\alpha_i\leq2, |\alpha|=d\},                 \tag{1.2}
\]

then

\[
 G_d=\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\mathcal B_d}
       k[P\times A]u^\alpha.                         \tag{1.3}
\]

Let (widehat A=\operatorname{Hom}(A,k^\times)).  Since
(|A|=4=1) in (k), the four Fourier projectors require no nontrivial
denominator:

\[
 e_\lambda=\sum_{a\in A}\lambda(a)[a],
 \qquad \lambda\in\widehat A.                       \tag{1.4}
\]

Thus

\[
 \boxed{
 G_d=\bigoplus_{(\lambda,\alpha)\in
       \widehat A\times\mathcal B_d} k[P]e_\lambda u^\alpha.} \tag{1.5}
\]

## 2. Source actors preserve every Fourier--monomial block

For a marked actor (ell), v443 gives

\[
 L_\ell([p,a]u^\alpha)
 =[p_\ell p,a_\ell+a]E(S(a)v_\ell)u^\alpha.          \tag{2.1}
\]

In the associated degree-(d) quotient, every positive-degree term of
(E(S(a)v_\ell)) raises (u^\alpha) into (I^{d+1}).  Hence

\[
 L_\ell([p,a]u^\alpha)
 \equiv[p_\ell p,a_\ell+a]u^\alpha\pmod {I^{d+1}}.  \tag{2.2}
\]

Fourier transforming the (A)-coordinate gives

\[
 \boxed{
 L_\ell(k[P]e_\lambda u^\alpha)
 \subseteq k[P]e_\lambda u^\alpha,}                 \tag{2.3}
\]

with scalar (lambda(a_\ell)) and the usual PSL left translation.  Thus
the transition-defect space (H_{d-1}) of v444 can be closed independently
in the (4h_d) source blocks indexed by ((\lambda,\alpha)).  Each complete
six-occurrence/two-component block has width

\[
 6\cdot2\cdot|P|=6,048,                              \tag{2.4}
\]

regardless of (d).  The decomposition is applied only after the defects
have been constructed from the full lower transition records; it is not the
unsound replacement (D_d=I^d\mathcal M).

## 3. Occurrences permute the blocks exactly

Let (M_j) be a signed permutation matrix.  Its degree-preserving leading
substitution sends

\[
 u^\alpha\longmapsto
 \varepsilon_j(\alpha)u^{\pi_j\alpha},
 \qquad \varepsilon_j(\alpha)\in\{1,2\},            \tag{3.1}
\]

where (pi_j) is the underlying coordinate permutation; quadratic terms
from a negative column have higher degree and do not enter (3.1).  The
crossed factor (E(c_j(a))) likewise contributes only its constant term on
the associated grade.  Combining (3.1) with the quotient action (A_j)
gives

\[
 \boxed{
 (\lambda,\alpha)\longmapsto
 (\lambda\circ A_j^{-1},\pi_j\alpha).}               \tag{3.2}
\]

The coefficient also receives the explicit sign
(arepsilon_j(\alpha)).  Formula (3.2) is exactly the higher-grade version
of the inverse-character transport in v439.  It retains a common source
label and sends its six occurrence components to their corresponding target
labels; six source blocks must not be initialized independently per tag.

A fixed left prefix at the target has the associated-grade form (2.2), so it
preserves the target pair and multiplies by the appropriate character scalar.
Therefore the signed six-occurrence physical map has nonzero blocks only
along the finite permutation graph generated by (3.2).

## 4. Pure-grade transport components and the necessary connection edges

Let (Omega_d=\widehat A\times\mathcal B_d).  Form an undirected graph by
joining (omega\in\Omega_d) to every image (3.2) under the six registered
occurrences.  Then source blocks belonging to distinct connected components
have disjoint target support **for pure new-grade rows**, while every coupling
of such rows forced by occurrence transport lies inside one component.

This graph alone need not decompose the complete physical fibre.  A lifted
old row contains lower-to-grade connection terms.  When a linear combination
of lifted old rows cancels in the lower physical block, its remaining grade
row can have support in more than one pure transport component.  Those
supports are coupled by one source coefficient and must not be solved
separately.

In degree one, the six underlying (pi_j) generate (S_3), and the
conjugation representation identifies the three coordinate sign characters
(eta_1,\eta_2,\eta_3) with the three nontrivial elements of
(widehat A).  The twelve pairs have exactly three **pure transport** components:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{component}&\text{pairs}&\text{physical width}\ \hline
C_{1}&(1,i),\ i=1,2,3&3\cdot2016=6048\\
C_{=}&(\eta_i,i),\ i=1,2,3&3\cdot2016=6048\\
C_{\ne}&(\eta,i),\ \eta\ne1,\ \eta\ne\eta_i&6\cdot2016=12096.
\end{array}                                           \tag{4.1}
\]

Here one target pair has width

\[
 2\text{ hexagons}\cdot2\text{ PB3 components}\cdot504=2016. \tag{4.2}
\]

The widths in (4.1) sum to 24,192, the v442 grade-one physical width.  They
are not asserted to remain three independent complete-fibre solves.
For degrees two through six, the graph has at most
(4h_d\leq28) vertices and is computed exhaustively from the six pinned
tables; no guessed representation-theoretic splitting is needed.

The safe full graph is obtained as follows.  Start with all pure occurrence
edges (3.2).  Perform v441 lower-first elimination on every lifted old basis
row.  For each resulting zero-lower grade row, add one hyperedge joining all
vertices of (\Omega_d) on which that row has nonzero support.  Replace a
hyperedge by a clique, or equivalently union all its vertices.  Call the
resulting graph (\mathscr G_d^{\rm full}).  Then and only then the complete
fibre is a direct sum over connected components of
(\mathscr G_d^{\rm full}): pure defect rows lie inside the old occurrence
components, and every connection row lies inside the component created by
its own support hyperedge.  This statement is tautologically fail-closed and
does not assume a parity separation for the actual connection.

## 5. Lower-to-grade connection terms remain present

The block invariance (2.3) applies to the pure new-grade defect closure.
It does **not** say that lifting an old lower basis creates no connection
term.  The positive-degree part of
(E(S(a)v_\ell)), the quadratic terms in negative substitutions, and the
crossed factors (E(c_j(a))) all contribute when a lower-degree row is
reevaluated one precision higher.

V444 retains these contributions in two places:

1. seed and transition defects are formed by full precision-(d) evaluation
   before projecting to their Fourier--monomial block;
2. every lifted old basis row is still fed to v441's lower-first physical
   echelon alongside the exhausted pure-grade defect basis.

Thus v445 is an exact source-block implementation of v444, not a
linearization that drops the return-even/field-outer connection.  In
particular, a degree-zero character can feed several grade-one
Fourier--monomial vertices through its connection row; this is precisely why
the extra hyperedges in Section 4 are mandatory.

## 6. Implementation consequence

For the first rung, a producer may maintain one sparse echelon per source
pair ((\lambda,\alpha)), each of ambient width 6,048.  It should:

1. replay every lower seed/transition equality;
2. construct each full lifted defect and prove its lower reduction is zero;
3. Fourier-project that defect and route each homogeneous component to the
   corresponding source block;
4. exhaust the four common actors independently within each block;
5. aggregate the retained rows through (3.2), including every sign and
   prefix scalar;
6. lower-reduce all lifted old rows, add their actual connection-support
   hyperedges, and solve physical membership only within the resulting
   components of (\mathscr G_d^{\rm full});
7. retain literal ancestry through both source and physical echelons.

At grade one this replaces one 72,576-coordinate occurrence echelon by
twelve 6,048-coordinate echelons.  On the physical side, (4.1) is the initial
partition; actual connection hyperedges may merge any of its three pieces,
up to the full 24,192-coordinate fibre.  Thus no unmeasured physical speedup
is promised.  The method does not reduce the registered universe or the
total coordinate count; it safely reduces source-side dense pivot interaction
and uses every physical separation that survives the actual connection rows.

The same proof applies separately at every first-rung grade.  It does not
apply unchanged to the second rung, whose quotient already contains the
nonsemisimple (C_3^3) rotation coordinate; no second-rung Fourier speedup is
claimed here.
