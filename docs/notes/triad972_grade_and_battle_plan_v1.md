$$A:=a_M(G_{\mathbf Q})\ \subseteq\ \mathcal{PR}_M(\widehat{GT}_{\rm gen})\ \subseteq\ GT(M),\qquad 648=\underbrace{\#\alpha}_{\text{fake}}+\underbrace{\#\beta}_{\text{genuine 非算術}}$$
